Legrand Valuation & acquisition analysis

I valued Legrand’s operating business, then asked how much it could pay for a hypothetical electrical equipment company and how much cash the target would need after closing.

Analysis by George Dharma · September 2026 operating base · Five-year forecast · Hypothetical acquisition

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

How much would I bid?

I would cap the bid at €489.1m on the operating plan. At the €500m ask, the target earns 9.48%. Legrand can fund the purchase, but the expected cash flows fall €11.1m short in present-value terms.

What the model values

The standalone DCF projects sales, margins, reinvestment and free cash flow from Legrand’s September 2026 financials. The target has its own five-year cash-flow forecast, savings ramp, integration costs and exit value. Its price is tested against a separate 10% required return.

Funding after the deal closes

The base case needs €15.6m of additional cash from Legrand over the first two years. Debt-service cover starts at 0.63×. I would make any price above the modelled ceiling contingent on the seller delivering the additional savings.
