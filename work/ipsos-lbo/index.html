Project 04 / Private equity · Sponsor returns

Ipsos Leveraged buyout analysis

I modelled a five-year buyout of Ipsos to find the entry price a sponsor could pay, given forecast cash generation, debt repayment and an explicit exit multiple.

Analysis by George Dharma · Hypothetical transaction · FY2025 accounts · Five-year sponsor holding period

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

What can the sponsor pay?

I would pass at €3.27bn. The base case turns €1.82bn of sponsor equity into €2.93bn of sale proceeds over five years. At the forecast earnings and debt levels, earning 20% at that entry price would require a 12.42× exit.

How the buyout is funded

I used FY2025 operating results to derive €363.4m of EBITDA after cash rent. The model follows senior and junior debt, a revolving facility and a €150m cash reserve through the hold. Exit proceeds allow for sale costs, pension claims and management participation.

Where the bid changes

At the assumed 8.5× exit, a 7.0× entry earns 21.4% and a 7.5× entry earns 17.8%. I would hold the bid to €2.61bn unless diligence identifies additional cash generation that the operating plan can support.
