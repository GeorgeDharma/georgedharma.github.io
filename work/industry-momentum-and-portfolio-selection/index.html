Project 11 / Python · Systematic investing

Industry momentum Sizing a momentum allocation

I tested how much of an equal-industry portfolio to allocate to recent winners. The comparison follows the additional return, active risk and industry concentration at four allocation sizes.

Analysis by George Dharma · 49 US industry research portfolios · 2010–2025 sizing study · Monthly rebalancing

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

How large a tilt would I take?

For a modest allocation I would start with 25% momentum and keep 75% in equal industry weights. It limited the largest industry to 4.03% at rebalance and took about a quarter of the tracking risk of the full momentum portfolio.

Selecting the rule and sizing the exposure

I ranked industries on eleven months of returns, skipping the most recent month. The unbuffered top-ten rule was selected using 2000–2009 data after development on 1970–1999. I then compared four allocation sizes on the same 192 months of the 2010–2025 test period.

How certain is the advantage?

The 25% mix added 0.43 percentage points a year on an arithmetic basis. A 95% interval from paired twelve-month block resampling runs from −0.28 to +1.15 points. The sizing analysis revisits the strategy test period, so its results are exploratory.
