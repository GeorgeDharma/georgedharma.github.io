Project 09 / Python · Interest-rate derivatives

Euro swap portfolioDesigning an interest-rate hedge

I built two euro interest-rate curves and repriced four hypothetical swaps. The decision was how much hedge notional to commit when maturities can move in different directions.

€95m portfolio · Open Source Risk sample market, 5 February 2016

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

What hedge size would I use?

At the €20m limit, the model receives fixed on €9.15m of five-year swaps and pays fixed on €10.85m of ten-year swaps. This gives the lowest maximum loss on the four additional shocks among the limits tested.

How the hedge was chosen

I calibrated an overnight discount curve and a six-month Euribor projection curve from 40 quotes. I repriced each trade under six design shocks, then chose hedge positions subject to a gross notional limit. Four more curve moves tested the resulting positions.

Why I stopped at €20m

A €60m limit almost removes the largest design loss, but a short-end shock costs €504k. Even the €20m hedge increases the loss when short rates rise and long rates fall. The choice depends on which curve moves the desk needs to withstand.
