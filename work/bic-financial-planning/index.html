Project 08 / FP&A · Cash forecasting

BIC Cash planning & performance analysis

I connected BIC’s sales performance, operating budget and working capital to a cash forecast, then tested how much room the buyback leaves if collections slow.

Analysis by George Dharma · FY2026 planning case · Reported first-half financials and nine-month sales

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

How much buyback can the plan support?

The base plan uses the full €40m annual ceiling. I would wait for the seasonal cash inflow before proposing more. September supplied a sales update, so I forecast fourth-quarter sales from September and second-half profit and cash from the June accounts.

From budget variance to cash

First-half revenue missed my illustrative budget by €91.7m, yet operating profit beat it by €1.8m. I separated the effects of volume and margins from operating costs, then tested how slower collections and higher inventories would affect the €250m cash floor.

Where liquidity gets tight

With other flows fixed, 60 extra collection days and 30 extra inventory days push base year-end cash €2.6m below the floor before borrowing. I would check the monthly collection and inventory profile before committing further cash.
