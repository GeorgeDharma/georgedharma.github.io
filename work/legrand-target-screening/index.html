Project 03 / M&A · Target screening

Legrand Acquisition target screening

I screened ten European power and cooling businesses for Legrand. Before treating any ranking as actionable, I tested which capabilities a buyer could actually acquire.

Analysis by George Dharma · Analyst-defined acquisition mandate · Six ranked candidates · No assumed seller agreement

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

Who merits the first approach?

I would approach HiRef about its precision cooling business and keep Centiel as the alternative in modular backup power. CONTEG ranks third, subject to identifying the power and data-centre assets that could be purchased separately.

How I tested the shortlist

I scored product fit, additional capability, commercial fit and execution. At weights of 25%, 25%, 20% and 30%, HiRef scores 4.25 out of 5, Centiel 3.70 and CONTEG 3.45. I then tested all 231 combinations in five-percentage-point steps, with each criterion between 10% and 40%.

First diligence question

I would establish who owns the cooling designs, control software and customer contracts, and which engineers would transfer. For Centiel, I would test when confirmed US orders convert into shipments and cash before giving them full value in a bid.
