Project 07 / Market risk · Model validation

Portfolio tail risk Validation of daily loss forecasts

I compared four ways to forecast extreme daily losses on a portfolio that rebalances to 60% US equities and 40% Treasury bills each day.

Analysis by George Dharma · 2006–2025 backtest · Value-at-risk and expected shortfall

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

Which model would I monitor?

I would use filtered historical simulation alongside a separate stress limit. It had the fewest 99% breaches and the lowest loss score among the four methods, yet the joint coverage and independence test still rejected it at 5%.

How I scored a miss

Each forecast uses only earlier returns. A breach occurs when the portfolio loses more than the forecast VaR. I checked the number and clustering of breaches and compared realised tail losses with expected shortfall. The filtered model rescales historical shocks for current volatility.

Where the forecast fell short

The filtered model had 37 breaches in 2006–2015 and 27 in 2016–2025. Across breach dates, the average realised loss was 1.60%, against 1.53% forecast expected shortfall. I would review clusters and the largest individual misses when setting limits.
