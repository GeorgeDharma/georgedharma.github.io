Project 06 / Leveraged finance · Credit underwriting

Ipsos Credit & downside analysis

I underwrote the same hypothetical Ipsos buyout from the lender’s side. Both packages face the same earnings paths, cash reserve and revolving facility.

Analysis by George Dharma · Hypothetical buyout financing · Debt service, liquidity and recovery

Read the analysis ↓Project files ↓

What financing could I support?

I would decline the €1.64bn proposal. A €1.09bn senior-only loan improves downside cover and delays the first severe cash breach by three years. Even the smaller loan needs an agreed liquidity backstop or a lower debt commitment in that stress.

Cash service and recoveries

I deducted tax, capex, working capital and restructuring from operating cash flow, then measured it against interest and scheduled principal. A separate distress sale tests recoveries after sale costs, pension claims, rescue funding and the revolver.

What happens in a distressed sale?

At 5.0× EBITDA in year two of the severe case, the proposed senior loan recovers 45.1% and the junior loan recovers nothing. The senior-only loan recovers 70.3% on the same business. Actual priority would depend on the loan documents.
