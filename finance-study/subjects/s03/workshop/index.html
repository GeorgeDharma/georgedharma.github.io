Professional workshop · S03

Buy-side quality of earnings and completion accounts for Bracken Service

A private acquirer is reviewing the €55.0m equity ask for Bracken Service at 31 December. Deliver a quality-of-earnings report and an indicative closing bridge by noon. The buyer’s agreed negotiation convention is 7.5 times verified trailing sustainable EBITDA. The purchase agreement separately settles actual eligible working capital against a €7.4m peg. All amounts below are €m. The fictional source extracts are the evidence available before the deadline.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Transaction services senior analyst
What to submit
A quality-of-earnings schedule, a cash-conversion check, a net-debt and working-capital bridge and an exceptions memorandum to the deal partner.

From inputs to decision

01

Tie the data perimeter before analyzing adjustments

A dashboard can include group sales between companies and omit a small acquired entity. The earnings review should start with the accounts defining what the buyer receives.

  1. Bridge €54.7m dashboard sales minus €0.9m internal activity plus €0.4m external division sales to €54.2m signed sales. Residual is zero before new findings.
  2. Archive the period and entity mapping. The later €0.8m cut-off correction is a diligence discovery, so keep the signed starting point and show the analytical correction on its own row.

Traceable opening items are €54.2m signed sales and €8.4m reported EBITDA.

02

Restate December delivery and its matched cost

A signed sale agreement and a verified February collection do not move the January transfer of control into December.

  1. Remove €0.8m December revenue and reverse €0.5m December cost. December gross profit and EBITDA fall €0.30m. Corrected historical sales are €53.4m.
  2. The same decision removes €0.8m from closing receivables and restores €0.5m of inventory, assuming the goods belonged to Bracken on 31 December. The counterpart balance changes are required even though this is a timing issue.

A €0.30m trailing EBITDA correction and a matched closing balance correction are documented against the delivery record.

03

Separate sustainable costs from genuine one-offs

Reversing unusual expenses while retaining unusual gains inflates a bid. Conversely, missing owner services must be costed if the company needs them after sale.

  1. Starting from €8.40m EBITDA, subtract €0.50m insurance gain and add back €0.30m one-time relocation. Deduct €0.25m replacement manager, €0.30m cut-off margin and €0.15m recurring warranty undercharge.
  2. Old payroll still embedded in the last twelve months is €0.24m × 6/12 = €0.12m. To sustain a year of consultant coverage, another €0.04m is needed beyond the six months already booked. Add €0.08m net.

The closing-quality schedule arrives at €7.58m verified trailing sustainable EBITDA, down €0.82m from reported earnings.

04

Test whether recurring earnings turn into spendable cash

EBITDA does not tell the buyer how much cash the business needs to maintain equipment or customer credit. A high multiple deserves an independent cash check.

  1. Deduct €1.10m cash tax, €1.40m ordinary capital spending and €0.88m recurring working-capital increase from €7.58m. Result is €4.20m before financing.
  2. Divide €4.20m by €7.58m for approximately 55.4% recurring cash conversion. The €0.88m flow over a year is a different measure from closing working capital compared with a delivery target.

Cash evidence supports €4.20m on the assumptions, while the contract’s multiple still applies to the €7.58m EBITDA measure.

05

Rebuild closing working capital using the agreement

The operating price assumes the buyer receives normal stock and customer balances. Closing adjustments need a consistent perimeter with the €7.4m benchmark.

  1. Correct receivables to €6.10m − €0.80m = €5.30m. Correct inventory to €4.30m + €0.50m = €4.80m. Remove €0.60m seller fees from operating payables to give €3.00m.
  2. Defined actual is €5.30m + €4.80m − €3.00m = €7.10m. Compared with €7.40m peg, this reduces price by €0.30m. Confirm the historical peg excludes the same seller fees.

A €0.30m working-capital shortfall carries into the equity bridge.

06

Confirm the cash and debt-like obligations

An invoice that the buyer must settle reduces shareholder proceeds, but including it again in operating payables duplicates the deduction.

  1. Eligible cash is €2.40m − €0.40m restricted = €2.00m. Closing debt-like items are €9.00m principal, €0.20m accrued interest excluded from that principal, and €0.60m seller transaction fees, totaling €9.80m.
  2. Reconcile €0.60m to the same invoices excluded from actual working capital. Check loan payoff letters establish whether interest is already in the pay-off balance and whether closing-date interest changes it.

Indicative net debt under the agreement is €7.80m.

07

Compute proceeds and isolate the open customer risk

The buyer needs a supported recommendation separate from a commercial issue lacking evidence for a single probability.

  1. Operating price is €7.58m × 7.5 = €56.85m. Add €2.00m cash, deduct €9.80m debt-like items and deduct the €0.30m working-capital shortfall to obtain €48.75m equity proceeds.
  2. Against the ask this implies €55.00m − €48.75m = €6.25m gap. If the separate customer’s full €0.40m recurring contribution disappears and closing items otherwise stay fixed, the downside indication falls by €3.00m to €45.75m.

Recommend €48.75m as current verified indication, with a €45.75m loss-of-customer scenario and a targeted renewal evidence request.

08

Prove the pack can be handed over

Another analyst should be able to reproduce the cash settlement without accepting a hidden add-back, period mismatch or duplicate payable.

  1. Tie all adjustments to unique evidence IDs, record whether each affects trailing earnings, forward expectations, actual working capital or closing debt, and show separate effects when the same transaction legitimately affects two measures.
  2. Confirm shareholder consideration equals operating value less net debt plus the working-capital adjustment. Re-run without the fee reclassification in working capital to quantify the €0.60m duplicate deduction error.

The deal partner receives an evidenced base, a quantified downside and an identified action for each open contractual judgment.

Completed document

Buy-side transaction services findings | Bracken Service

Price conclusion

Our €48.75m indicative equity price is €6.25m below the seller’s ask using the buyer’s 7.5-times trailing EBITDA convention. Verified sustainable EBITDA is €7.58m. Recurring cash before financing is €4.20m on the stated spending assumptions.

Most consequential evidence

The €0.80m December sale did not transfer control until January. Historical sales fall to €53.40m and December gross profit falls €0.30m. February collection supports recoverability, while the delivery evidence governs the historic period.

Purchase agreement instructions

Set the working-capital measurement perimeter consistently with the €7.40m peg. Treat €0.60m seller fees as debt-like and remove them from supplier payables. Exclude €0.40m restricted cash and verify separately stated €0.20m accrued interest against lender confirmations.

Unresolved commercial exposure

The potential cancellation removes up to €0.40m annual contribution or €3.00m at the case multiple. We have no signed notice and no validated probability. Request renewal correspondence, cancellation dates and customer-level margin before altering the base or agreeing definitive contractual protection.
