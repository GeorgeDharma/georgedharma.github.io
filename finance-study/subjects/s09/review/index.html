New and expanded lessons

S09.11

Choosing between a lit book, a midpoint cross and a dealer quote

S09.01 introduces order books and requests for quotes. S09.03 considers passive execution and S09.10 compares accessible routes. A decision still needs a conditional non-fill price and an explicit break-even probability for a dark midpoint cross against a firm dealer quote.

  • Compare cost, execution certainty and information exposure across three venue mechanisms.
  • Derive the minimum crossing probability that justifies waiting under a stated deadline.

S09.12

Market-abuse surveillance and evidential escalation

S09.10 reconstructs best execution and broker conflicts. A different control asks whether orders or transactions warrant a confidential suspicion report. This chapter adds order-level triage, corroboration, attempted conduct and testing of alert quality.

  • Distinguish an unusual message pattern, reasonable suspicion and a legal finding.
  • Reconstruct the order evidence and assess false positives and missed events without treating an alert score as proof.
