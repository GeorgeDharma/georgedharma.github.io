Professional workshop · S09

The 500 shares missing from the broker's execution report

Prepare the order-level transaction-cost analysis for a fictional fund's 5,000-share purchase in Helix Water. The broker says its executions beat market VWAP. The portfolio manager says the delayed, incomplete purchase harmed the fund. Reconstruct both claims from the data, respect the price limit and review a disputed final quote. Use buyer costs as positive. All timestamps are on the same synchronized clock and share prices are in euros.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Buy-side execution analyst reviewing a portfolio manager's equity order
What to submit
Submit an execution-quality report to the head trader, portfolio manager and best-execution committee. The completed record must distinguish the manager's decision delay, desk implementation, spread and market movement, and the evidence needed to challenge one missed fill. No external market data are needed.

From inputs to decision

01

Freeze the correct paper portfolio

A valid comparison starts when the client decides to acquire the asset, even though responsibility for a delay can later be assigned elsewhere.

  1. At 09:59 the paper portfolio would own 5,000 shares at €40.00, an intended notional of €200,000. Preserve this benchmark when converting total shortfall to basis points.
  2. The desk could first act at 10:00 with midpoint €40.08. The client forbids a purchase above €40.22, accepts partial fills and sets an endpoint at 10:05. The endpoint €40.30 is a measurement price, not permission to execute at that level.

Use the investment decision for full implementation shortfall and arrival for the desk-only bridge. Keep the two timestamps and the limit in the case record.

02

Build the fill ledger and VWAP control

Before discussing poor performance, reconcile quantities, cash and fees with trade confirmations.

  1. The three actual fills cost €60,150, €80,280 and €40,200 respectively. Gross filled consideration is €180,630 on 4,500 shares, giving an average price of €40.14. Total explicit fees are €27 and filled cash cost is €180,657.
  2. Filled quantity is 90% of intended shares. The average fill beats the stated market VWAP by €0.03 per filled share, or €135 before fees and €108 after the stated fees if that benchmark bears none.

The broker's arithmetic regarding its fill average is correct. It omits the 500 shares that the client intended to own and any move before the trading window.

03

Calculate decision-level implementation shortfall

The paper and actual portfolios must be compared on the same amount of intended exposure, including the unfilled portion.

  1. Filled excess cost above €40.00 is 1,500 × €0.10 + 2,000 × €0.14 + 1,000 × €0.20 = €630. Missing 500 shares during a €0.30 rise creates €150 opportunity cost at the specified endpoint.
  2. Adding €27 fees gives total €807. Divide by €200,000 and multiply by 10,000 for 40.35 basis points. A fill marked at the endpoint rather than bought creates a measuring entry only.

The investment decision underperformed its paper portfolio by €807 even as the executed volume beat market VWAP. Both statements can be true without an error in either benchmark.

04

Assign pre-arrival and desk-period movements

Responsibility depends on who could take action at each timestamp. A decision-to-arrival bridge using the entire intended quantity must use arrival prices for all later components.

  1. Decision delay cost is 5,000 × (€40.08 − €40.00) = €400. Post-arrival execution costs are €30, €120 and €120 on the three fills, summing to €270.
  2. Post-arrival opportunity cost is 500 × (€40.30 − €40.08) = €110. With €27 fees the desk-period result is €407. Adding €400 earlier delay gives the €807 control total.

Obtain a timestamp for release of the completed order. The report identifies a €400 pre-arrival move but cannot alone determine whether that minute was avoidable.

05

Distinguish a spread crossing from a subsequent midpoint move

Spread metrics evaluate a local execution price relative to quotes near that fill. They do not equal implementation shortfall over the manager's whole horizon.

  1. Each fill paid €0.02 above its own pre-fill midpoint, giving a doubled effective spread of €0.04 per share. The one-way aggregate crossing cost is €0.02 × 4,500 = €90.
  2. The respective five-minute midpoint moves are +€0.04, +€0.01 and −€0.02. Multiplying by quantities gives +€60, +€20 and −€20, or +€60 total one-way movement. The remaining realized one-way spreads for liquidity suppliers sum to €30, reconciling €90 = €60 + €30.

The inferred impact numbers are descriptive. Later issuer news, market trends or order flow can move midpoints during those five minutes, including after the order's own expiry.

06

Test the disputed last quote as a feasible counterfactual

A quote at €40.24 is outside the hard limit however urgently the investor would like to own the shares. Only 100 shares were advertised inside the limit at 10:04:58.

  1. If venue A's 100 shares could actually be reached at €40.21 before expiry, buying them substitutes a 21-cent decision execution loss for a 30-cent missed-position loss. The improvement is 100 × €0.09 − €0.60 fee = €8.40.
  2. The remaining 400 at B cost €40.24 and cannot be assumed filled. Under the permissible 100-share scenario total shortfall becomes €798.60. Actual fill rate would rise to 92% without changing any earlier fill.

Request venue sequence numbers, order routing timestamps, access status and the quote's actual cancel or trade event. Until those arrive, identify a conditional €8.40 opportunity rather than a confirmed broker failure.

07

Turn the evidence into an execution decision

Client constraints and controllable actions belong next to the figures. Simply forcing a bigger final trade could violate the mandate.

  1. For future similar orders, test an earlier partial release or an agreed scheduling change to reduce the €400 decision delay. Pretrade execution models should show expected impact, outstanding shares and the cost of price drifting through the hard limit.
  2. Ask the broker to report full order completion, fees, decision and arrival benchmarks, and unfilled endpoint marks alongside market VWAP. Compare similar orders before drawing a general routing conclusion.

Escalate the final quote only if the event reconstruction shows accessible quantity long enough for a feasible order. Preserve the cost benchmark even if the last price was unavailable in tradeable size.

Completed document

Transaction-cost analysis and best-execution review

Order outcome

Purchased 4,500 of 5,000 shares within the €40.22 cap. Execution price averaged €40.14 versus market VWAP €40.17. Whole-order decision shortfall was €807, or 40.35 basis points, including the missing shares and fees.

Responsibility

€400 accrued before desk arrival. The desk-period implementation cost was €407, including €110 on 500 unfilled shares and €27 fees. Review the client release and routing timeline before allocating avoidable costs.

Market-quality finding

Each fill crossed its contemporaneous midpoint by €0.02. Five-minute midpoint changes account for €60 of €90 aggregate one-way crossing cost. This arithmetic has no automatic implication about private information or poor execution.

Routing decision

A 100-share quote on A could improve shortfall by at most €8.40 on the stipulated counterfactual. B's 400-share quote was outside the mandate. Seek a timestamped reconstruction and record the result before closing the exception.

Policy change

Retain decision, arrival and full-quantity reports next to market VWAP. Monitor missed-position cost and completion for genuinely comparable client instructions. Any future relaxation of the price limit requires the client's authorization.

Run a different case

See the full solution

Desk-period shortfall falls by €8.40 from €407. It is €398.60. Equivalently, execution adds €13, missed cost falls €22, and fees rise €0.60.

Task 3

If the order were released with the same fills and endpoint but its recorded valid decision midpoint were €40.03 instead of €40.00, what would total shortfall be?

See the full solution

All 5,000 intended shares benefit from a benchmark €0.03 higher. The difference is €150, reducing the full shortfall from €807 to €657. Keep the original decision record unless the new price is genuinely substantiated.
