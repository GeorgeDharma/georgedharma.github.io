Financial Econometrics and Empirical Research

A convincing finance study must specify what was known, what comparison answers the question and how uncertain the result is. A well-estimated association can still have the wrong causal interpretation.

Subject worked example

An announcement-day residual

A bank earns −1.5% on the event day. The market earns −2.0%. A pre-event model estimates zero intercept and beta 1.2.

Show the working

Expected return is 1.2 × (−2.0%) = −2.4%. Abnormal return is −1.5% − (−2.4%) = +0.9 percentage points. The share still fell in absolute terms. A single residual gives no significance test.

Course overview

What you will learn

Specify an estimand, estimate a model, diagnose its errors and assess robustness across plausible benchmarks, windows and samples.

Theory and assumptions

Ordinary least squares, panel methods, time-series dynamics and event studies. The estimator, identification assumption and uncertainty estimate are distinct choices that must fit the question.
