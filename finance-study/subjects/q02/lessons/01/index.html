Define the economic question

Specify the population, unit, time window and parameter of interest. A forecast, a description and a causal effect require different evidence.

The starting point

“Do higher rates hurt banks?” could refer to bank profits, share prices or lending volumes. Start by naming the population, unit of observation, dates and quantity to estimate. An average change in publicly listed bank shares can answer a different question from the change in lending at the typical bank.

Distinguish description, prediction and causal explanation. Description summarizes measured outcomes. Prediction asks whether information available today helps anticipate a later outcome. A causal claim additionally needs a credible comparison with what would have happened to the same units under another policy or exposure.

Theory and method

Potential outcomes and selection bias

Rubin causal model · Average treatment effect on the treated

Banks using a guarantee approve 15 percentage points more applications. Which part of that gap can you interpret as the guarantee’s effect?

A borrower approved under a guarantee has a realized outcome and a missing outcome under ordinary lending. Comparing guaranteed banks with other banks also compares their customers, sectors and pre-existing screening practices. The observed approval gap can be split into the causal change for participating banks and the difference that would already have existed without the guarantee. Defining that missing outcome makes the empirical task precise before selecting an estimator.

How the result is built

E[Y|D=1]−E[Y|D=0] = ATT + E[Y(0)|D=1]−E[Y(0)|D=0]

D indicates participation, Y observed approval outcome, Y(1) and Y(0) potential outcomes with and without the guarantee, ATT = E[Y(1)−Y(0)|D=1], and E the population mean. For binary approval, differences are approval-rate points.

  1. Consistency maps observed Y to Y(1) for participants and Y(0) for nonparticipants.
  2. Add and subtract participants’ unobserved no-guarantee mean. The first difference is ATT, the second the pre-existing outcome gap under no treatment.
  3. If the illustrative means are 80% for treated, 70% for their untreated counterfactual and 65% for controls, then 15 points equals ten treatment points plus five selection points.

When the reasoning holds

  • The potential outcomes require a well-defined treatment version. A guarantee with free underwriting advice differs from a guarantee alone.
  • Assume no cross-bank spillovers for this simple notation. Market-wide competition effects can change controls’ outcomes and require a richer design.

First example

Two banks worth €90m and €10m return +2% and −4%. The average return across banks is (2 − 4)/2 = −1%. An investor holding both in proportion to starting market values earns 0.9 × 2% + 0.1 × (−4%) = 1.4%. Each figure answers a distinct question.

Specify the estimand

An analyst asks whether foreign banks cut lending when exchange rates fall. Before opening a spreadsheet, define an estimand, the population quantity that would answer the question if suitable observations existed. Total lending in euros, the typical bank’s local-currency growth and the number of borrowers losing access are different quantities.

The unit can be a borrower, a bank or a country-month. Choosing it determines which observations receive equal weight and where dependence arises. A sample containing only banks still reporting at year-end may silently change the population.

Distinguish change from comparison

A descriptive estimand might be average lending growth among observed foreign banks during a depreciation. For prediction, specify the forecast horizon and the information available at issue time. A causal estimand compares potential outcomes with and without an exposure for a defined group.

Only one potential outcome is observed per bank on each date. A credible design must explain how comparison units or external variation provide information on the missing outcome. Accurate measurement of the realized outcome alone leaves that counterfactual unresolved.

Define aggregation in advance

The percentage growth of a sum weights each entity’s growth by its initial balance. The simple average weights entities equally. Derive the first relation by writing each change as initial balance times its growth and dividing total change by total initial balance.

Currency translation provides another source of change. A bank issuing the same number of local-currency loans can report a smaller euro book if its currency falls. Define whether the intended activity measure includes or removes that movement.

Method

Write a research specification

  1. Set the boundary

    Specify the target population, dates and exposure definition before inspecting subgroup results.

  2. Write the numerator and denominator

    Choose whether each bank, customer or euro carries equal weight and define the treatment of missing observations.

  3. State the inference

    Describe, forecast or identify a causal effect. Record the comparison required by the selected purpose.

Worked case

A cross-border loan book

Two foreign subsidiaries begin with local loan balances equivalent to €80m and €20m. At constant exchange rates their balances grow 5% and fall 10%. Exchange rates are frozen for this calculation.

Working

  1. The first book gains €4m and the second loses €2m. The group gains €2m on €100m, giving aggregate growth of 2%.
  2. The equal-subsidiary mean is (5% − 10%)/2 = −2.5%. It describes the average subsidiary’s growth rather than group volume.
  3. The initial-weight calculation 0.8 × 5% + 0.2 × (−10%) reproduces +2%. Neither calculation supplies what growth would have been without a depreciation.

Interpretation

For an investor studying volume consolidated on group accounts, the 2% measure is relevant after specifying translation. For research on an average institution, −2.5% may be the estimand.

A common mistake

A bigger sample automatically fixes a mismatch between the measured outcome and the question.

How to correct it

Increasing a sample can reduce noise around the wrong population quantity. Check the units, denominator, currency conversion and timing before discussing precision.

Quick check
You want the one-day return on all listed bank equity held in starting market-value proportions. Which summary fits?

Market-value weights reproduce that portfolio’s change in wealth. Selecting profitable banks changes the population, and a count discards the size of each price move.

Independent exam problem

Two banks start with 60 and 40 units of deposits and finish with 66 and 36. An analyst attributes all change to an advertising campaign.

  1. Part A · 4 marks

    Calculate aggregate and equal-bank growth.

  2. Part B · 4 marks

    What additional evidence would a causal claim need?

Reveal the worked marking guide

Part A 4 marks

Initial-weight aggregate growth is (102 − 100)/100 = 2%. Individual growth is +10% and −10%, giving equal-bank growth zero. State the weighting difference for full credit.

Part B 4 marks

Specify which banks or customers received the campaign and how deposits would have changed otherwise. A credible comparable group or exogenous rollout could support identification if its required assumptions hold.

Interview practice

Explain it aloud

Banks using a guarantee approve 15 percentage points more applications. Which part of that gap can you interpret as the guarantee’s effect?

Saved on this device as you type.
Compare with a spoken answer

I can report the 15-point descriptive difference immediately. To label any of it causal I need a design for participants’ missing approval rate without the guarantee. If credible evidence placed that rate at 70%, the treated effect would be ten points. Random assignment, a justified comparison trend or defensible conditional exchangeability can supply different routes to that missing quantity.

Interviewer’s follow-up

Does randomizing invitations identify the effect of actually taking the guarantee?

Compare with the follow-up answer

Randomizing the offer identifies an intention-to-treat effect under the relevant design conditions. To identify an effect of take-up one also needs a first stage and assumptions such as exclusion and monotonicity for a complier effect, or perfect compliance for the simpler interpretation.
