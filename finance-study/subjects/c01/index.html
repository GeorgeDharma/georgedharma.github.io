Subject worked example

Who funds the investment?

In a simplified economy national saving is 18% of output and investment is 23%. Use the common current-account identity.

Show the working

The current account is 18% − 23% = −5% of output. The deficit indicates net borrowing in this example. Its desirability depends partly on what the investment produces.

Course overview

What you will learn

Reconcile external flows, explain exchange-rate regimes and trace an interest-rate or commodity shock through a country’s balance sheet.

Theory and assumptions

The saving–investment identity, intertemporal trade and the open-economy policy trilemma. National accounts identify what must add up. Behavioural models explain why it changes.
