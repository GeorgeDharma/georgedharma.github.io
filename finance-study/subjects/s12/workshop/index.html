Professional workshop · S12

Harbour Engineers Pension Plan annual investment committee paper

The trustees of the fictional Harbour Engineers plan need their 2025 results before a 12 January 2026 meeting. Members receive promised benefits, so both the investment outcome and the change in the funding deficit matter. The chair also asks for an executable rebalance and a decision on a conflicted shareholder vote. Work in euro millions unless stated. All returns are total returns and both halves span equal lengths of time for the money-weighted illustration.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Portfolio performance and investment governance officer for a defined-benefit pension trustee
What to submit
Submit a signed committee paper with a reconciled custody and actuarial exhibit, return attribution, a trade instruction with a cash reserve, and an independently reviewed proxy instruction. The trustees can adopt or amend each decision at the meeting.

From inputs to decision

01

Rebuild both sides of the June cut-off

A snapshot taken after the sponsor contribution would make a deposit look like manager performance. The actual investment mark belongs immediately before both external flows.

  1. Shares earn €55m × 10% = €5.500m. Bonds/cash earn €45m × 1% = €0.450m. Gross assets become €105.950m. Deduct the €0.100m fee to establish the €105.850m pre-flow mark.
  2. The sponsor sends €10.000m and the administrator pays €5.000m to beneficiaries. Their net external inflow is €5.000m. The second half begins at €110.850m. Keep both gross cash entries for reconciliation with the actuary.

Carry €105.850m as June’s performance endpoint and €110.850m as the capital invested in the second half.

02

Close the custody ledger and calculate manager returns

Trustees need the investment result after costs, the policy-relative gross result on comparable inputs, and a closing value reconcilable to actual assets.

  1. Second-half gross investment loss is €110.850m × 2% = €2.217m. Subtract that and the €0.100m second fee to get €108.533m. The full-year asset bridge is 100 + 5.950 − 2.217 − 0.200 + 10.000 − 5.000 = 108.533.
  2. Gross time-weighted return compounds as 1.0595 × 0.98 − 1 = 3.831%. The net calculation links marks after each fee, (105.850/100) × (108.533/110.850) − 1 = 3.6375%. Linking means the sponsor’s choice of flow size does not set the relative weight of the two returns.

Policy compound return is 1.056 × 0.99 − 1 = 4.544%. Gross underperformance was 0.713 percentage point. Net return was 0.9065 point below the stated gross policy benchmark, with that mixed fee basis explicitly labeled.

03

Attribute the first half to actual investment decisions

A satisfactory committee report must explain why the first half beat policy even while the portfolio held less of the better-performing asset class.

  1. First-half policy is 0.60 × 8% + 0.40 × 2% = 5.60%, compared with the actual 5.95%. Under Brinson–Fachler, allocation is (0.55 − 0.60) × (8% − 5.60%) = −0.12 point in shares and (0.45 − 0.40) × (2% − 5.60%) = −0.18 point in bonds.
  2. Apply the security selection spreads to policy weights. Shares contribute 0.60 × 2% = +1.20 points and bonds 0.40 × (−1%) = −0.40 point. Interaction uses active weights times selection spreads and contributes −0.10 and −0.05 point respectively.

The check is −0.30 allocation + 0.80 selection − 0.15 interaction = +0.35 point. Second-half gross relative return was −1.00 point. A sector attribution for that half needs beginning sector holdings and sector returns, so the annual paper confines sector explanations to the evidenced first half.

04

Measure the member’s invested-money experience

The sponsor committed additional money immediately before the losing half. A return that weights each euro by when it was supplied answers a different question from manager comparison.

  1. Solve 100(1 + r) + 5√(1 + r) = 108.533 for the annual rate r. Opening capital compounds for a year and the additional net €5m compounds for half a year. Setting x = √(1 + r) gives 100x² + 5x − 108.533 = 0.
  2. The positive root gives r = 3.4475%. A cross-check is Modified Dietz, using weighted invested capital 100 + 0.5 × 5 = 102.5. The gain 108.533 − 100 − 5 = 3.533 divided by 102.5 gives 3.4468%.

The capital-weighted result falls below the 3.6375% net linked result because additional capital was present for the loss. Both use the same investments, fees and custody cash flows.

05

Bridge the funding deficit and isolate the valuation change

An asset benchmark can be beaten or missed while the plan’s ability to cover benefits moves in either direction. The actuary and custodian must use the same population and benefit payments.

  1. Closing liabilities are 110 + 5.500 interest + 2.000 service + 4.000 revision − 5.000 benefits = €116.500m. The closing deficit is 116.500 − 108.533 = €7.967m, compared with €10.000m at opening.
  2. Net investment gain €3.533m plus €10.000m contributions less €11.500m of liability growth before payments improves surplus by €2.033m. The €5.000m benefit payment cancels from this surplus bridge. Funding rises from 100/110 = 90.91% to 108.533/116.5 = 93.16%.

The assumption revision alone added €4.000m to the obligation. The committee can ask the actuary what changed without assigning the valuation movement to the portfolio manager.

06

Size and release the post-close rebalance

The custodian’s final 64% equity exceeds the 63% upper limit. Policy calls for restoring the 60% target after trade costs and reserving the next benefit cheque.

  1. At the close equities are 0.64 × 108.533 = €69.46112m. If x is the equity sold in €m, fund value after costs is 108.533 − 0.001x and equity is 69.46112 − x. Setting their ratio to 0.60 gives x = 4.34132/(1 − 0.0006) = €4.343926m.
  2. The €4,344 switch cost leaves €4.339582m proceeds. Keep €2.000m of that in cash, topping up available cash from €2.000m to €4.000m. Buy €2.339582m of the approved bond allocation. Net equity €65.117194m is 60.00% of the €108.528656m post-trade total.

Issue a paired sale and bond/cash instruction. Operations checks actual execution cost and the settled €4.000m reserve against these targets. Record the January transaction in the following period.

07

Resolve the vote and assign the records

The sponsor relationship can affect how the decision looks to members, even if the investment return calculations are correct. Use the plan’s stated authority and evidence threshold.

  1. Record that a sponsor director sits on the investee board and that the independent valuation required by policy is absent. Exclude that director from deliberation and record the independent chair’s decision to oppose the proposed related-party sale.
  2. Instruct the proxy agent to vote all 120,000 eligible plan shares against the resolution. The instruction is only an order until the agent supplies execution confirmation. Request the issuer’s independent valuation and place it on the next stewardship agenda.

The chair signs the voting rationale and operations reconciles eligible, instructed and confirmed votes. The member communication can report the instruction immediately and the actual voting result after confirmation.

Completed document

Investment committee decision paper — submitted by the performance and governance officer

Performance minute

For 2025, report +3.831% gross and +3.638% net time-weighted against +4.544% gross policy. First-half security selection added 0.80 point at policy weights, overcoming negative allocation and interaction. Second-half gross loss exceeded policy loss by one point. Request sector data from the manager to explain that period before drawing a manager-retention conclusion.

Member funding minute

Assets are €108.533m against €116.500m promised benefits. The funding deficit narrowed from €10.000m to €7.967m. Sponsor contributions were €10.000m and the liability assumption change added €4.000m. Report the 3.448% money-weighted net return separately from the 93.16% funded ratio.

Trading authority

Approve an estimated €4.343926m equity sale, retain €2.000m additional benefit cash and allocate the remaining €2.339582m proceeds after cost to approved bonds. Operations will release the paired orders, check settlement, and escalate any inability to meet the €4.000m cash reserve.

Voting authority

The independent chair instructs a vote against the related-party sale on 120,000 shares. The sponsor director is recused. The issuer has supplied no independent valuation required by the plan. Record the agent’s execution confirmation and request valuation evidence before the next engagement review.

Sign-off and ownership

Performance officer reconciled custody calculations. The actuary owns the benefit remeasurement explanation. The trustee chair approves the vote and trade authority, the operations lead verifies settlement and the secretary records the committee decision and any qualification.

Run a different case

Use the changed facts in each task. Enter numbers in the units shown, using a decimal point or comma and no thousands separators.

Task 1

If the second-half actual gross return had been −1.00% with the two fees and all other inputs unchanged, what would closing assets be in €m?

See the full solution

The second half still opens at €110.850m. Its revised gross loss is €1.1085m. Deduct that and the €0.100m closing fee to obtain €109.6415m. First-half performance and the sponsor cash flows are unchanged.

Task 2

If the actuary removes the entire €4.000m upward revision and actual assets remain €108.533m, what is the year-end funded ratio in percent?

See the full solution

The revised obligation is 116.500 − 4.000 = €112.500m. Divide 108.533 by 112.500 and multiply by 100 to obtain 96.4738%. The asset manager’s return stays as reported because this counterfactual changes the liability estimate.

Task 3

Assume the unchanged €108.533m portfolio closes at 66.00% shares. Under the same 60% post-cost target, how many €m of equity must the desk sell?

See the full solution

Excess equity before costs is (0.66 − 0.60) × 108.533 = €6.51198m. Divide by 1 − 0.60 × 0.001 = 0.9994, giving €6.515890m. The slight increase over the pre-cost excess reflects the lower capital base after execution.

Task 4

The proxy agent reports that only 100,000 shares were voted. How should the independent chair present the result to members?

Report 120,000 eligible and instructed against, 100,000 confirmed against and 20,000 unconfirmed or unvoted pending reconciliation. That is an 83.33% confirmed vote rate on eligible shares. Ask operations and the agent to investigate the shortfall, record the deadline and outcome, preserve the recusal, and update the record when the status is established. Do not describe all 120,000 votes as completed.
