Investment Management and Performance Evaluation

A portfolio can beat the stock market and still fail its owner if a pension payment falls due at the wrong time. Investment management begins with the investor’s liabilities, constraints and benchmark.

Subject worked example

Currency changes the result

An overseas share gains 10% in local currency, while that currency falls 5% against the investor’s home currency. Ignore fees and hedging.

Show the working

Home-currency return is 1.10 × 0.95 − 1 = 4.5%. The interaction term explains why simply subtracting 5 from 10 gives a different result.

Course overview

What you will learn

Translate a mandate into holdings, choose a benchmark and reconcile performance while distinguishing manager skill from exposures and client cash-flow timing.

Theory and assumptions

Strategic asset allocation, liability matching, active risk and performance attribution. Results depend on the benchmark, investment horizon and the currency in which liabilities are paid.
