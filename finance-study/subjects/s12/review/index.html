New and expanded lessons

S12.11

Suitability, client capacity and conflicted product advice

S12.01 establishes investor objectives and S12.10 covers stewardship conflicts. Product advice adds an individual client fact-find, a quantitative check against the whole portfolio, compensation disclosure and a decision when the smallest available trade exceeds the client’s constraints.

  • Translate verified spending, concentrations and stress tolerance into a maximum suitable trade under a written mandate.
  • Document the advisory role, fees, product constraints and an independently supported recommendation.

S12.12

Fair dealing and allocation of partially filled block orders

S09.10 reviews execution quality and S12.10 examines a trustee’s conflicted vote. Neither shows how a manager allocates a secondary-market block fill among several eligible client accounts without rewarding fee levels, timing decisions after prices move or favoring house interests.

  • Apply a documented pre-trade allocation policy to eligible accounts and reconcile an average execution price and costs.
  • Identify how mandate differences, rounding and post-trade changes should be governed.

S12.13

Private-fund commitments, capital calls and liquidity pacing

S05.10 already teaches private-fund performance and a dated public-market equivalent. The allocator also has to decide how much to promise a new fund while existing managers can call capital and public markets may fall before distributions arrive.

  • Reconcile liquid assets, private NAV and unfunded commitments without confusing a promise with an investment.
  • Size a new commitment under base and stressed cash and allocation limits.

Standard III(B) Fair Dealing

The guidance addresses fair treatment across clients, disclosure of allocation procedures, and equitable treatment of eligible clients when investment opportunities or fills are limited.
