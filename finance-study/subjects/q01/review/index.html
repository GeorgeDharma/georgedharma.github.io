New and expanded lessons

Q01.11

Generate correlated risk scenarios with Cholesky factors

Q01.06 uses covariance eigenvectors to study risk and Q01.05 solves exposure systems. The new exercise constructs a triangular covariance factor, uses it to manufacture a joint shock and verifies the variance of a traded combination.

  • Factor a two-asset covariance matrix by matching its entries.
  • Generate a dependent pair of shocks from independent draws and validate portfolio variance.

Q01.12

Invert a market quote with a safeguarded root solver

Q01.02 develops local price sensitivity and Q04.08 uses a Newton update for implied volatility. This chapter makes a price bracket, proves a yield-error bound after repeated bisection, and tests both quote residual and solution uniqueness.

  • Translate a market calibration into a signed residual with an admissible domain.
  • Combine a guaranteed interval with Newton acceleration and distinguish price error from yield error.
