Mathematical Methods for Quantitative Finance

The same mathematics describes how a bond reacts to rates, how holdings combine into a portfolio and how to choose weights under constraints. Begin each method with a small financial example before introducing general notation.

Subject worked example

A minimum-variance allocation

Assets A and B have variances 0.04 and 0.01 and zero covariance. Invest weight w in A and 1 − w in B, fully invested without short selling.

Show the working

Variance is 0.04w² + 0.01(1 − w)². Set its slope 0.10w − 0.02 to zero to get w = 0.20. Variance is 0.008 and volatility is about 8.94%.

Course overview

What you will learn

Differentiate a value function, express portfolio risk in matrix form and solve a constrained optimization while checking its assumptions.

Theory and assumptions

Linear algebra, multivariable calculus and convex optimization. A covariance matrix must produce nonnegative variance for every portfolio. Convexity makes many optimization problems tractable.
