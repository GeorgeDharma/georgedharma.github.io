Q04Quantitative finance

Numerical Pricing, Interest-Rate Models and Model Validation

A pricing algorithm can return a number with many decimal places even when it solves the wrong contract or uses an unstable approximation. Numerical finance studies both the method and the evidence for trusting it.

Subject worked example

Is the difference numerical noise?

The mean discounted payoff from 10,000 independent simulated paths is €4.96 and its sample standard deviation is €5. A closed-form result is €5.00.

Show the working

Standard error is 5/√10,000 = €0.05. A large-sample 95% interval is about €4.862–€5.058. The benchmark lies inside the interval. Check discretization error separately.

Course overview

What you will learn

Price a contract by independent methods, quantify numerical error, calibrate to a curve or option surface and write a validation conclusion with limits on use.

Theory and assumptions

Risk-neutral expectation, partial differential equations, lattice methods and calibration. Sampling error, discretization error and model error have different causes and need separate checks.
