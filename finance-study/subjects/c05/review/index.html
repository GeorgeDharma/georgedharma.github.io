New and expanded lessons

C05.11

Building an equity index and maintaining its divisor

The portfolio and performance courses use a benchmark and identify capitalization weighting. They do not run the same two securities through alternative published index rules or preserve an index level at a split.

  • Calculate price-weighted, equal-weighted and free-float capitalization-weighted returns from identical stocks.
  • Adjust a price-weighted index divisor so a stock split creates no artificial return.

C05.12

Fund NAV and the ETF creation and redemption mechanism

Diversification, index exposures and active weights were covered. Learners had yet to reconcile the assets behind a pooled share with an executable ETF price or estimate a creation trade after basket and market costs.

  • Compute per-share net asset value and an exchange premium.
  • Test a creation or redemption using executable prices, the permitted basket and costs.
