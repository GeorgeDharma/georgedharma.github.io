C05Core finance

Investments, Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing

Two risky assets can form a safer portfolio if their bad periods differ. Asset pricing asks which risks remain after diversification and why investors require compensation for them.

Subject worked example

Diversification by hand

Two assets each have 20% volatility. Hold half in each. Their return correlation is zero.

Show the working

Variance is 0.5² × 0.2² + 0.5² × 0.2² = 0.02. Volatility is √0.02 = 14.14%. At correlation one, the same portfolio has 20% volatility.

Course overview

What you will learn

Derive portfolio risk, estimate a market beta and distinguish a pricing model’s prediction from an observed realized return.

Theory and assumptions

Mean–variance theory, CAPM, factor pricing and market efficiency. Conclusions depend on the market portfolio, information set and constraints investors face.
