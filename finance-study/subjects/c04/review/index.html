New and expanded lessons

C04.11

Value a closely held company when the founder leaves

Peer beta and public multiples are already worked, and transaction diligence normalizes owner compensation. This lesson joins those tools to an explicit founder-departure scenario and a restricted minority stake, showing which effects belong in operating cash and which attach to the security itself.

  • Normalize owner-managed earnings for an economically equivalent replacement team.
  • Value dependent customer relationships and a specific restriction without charging the same loss twice.

C04.12

Build and interpret comparable acquisition multiples

Public trading comparisons, bargaining, share consideration and price bridges are established. The earlier units do not assemble a table of historical completed deals on one date, consideration and lease basis and use the resulting range without applying a second generic control premium.

  • Convert the amounts paid to sellers into a comparable whole-business value for each acquisition.
  • Normalize acquisition multiples, identify embedded buyer benefits and explain a range for a new target.

C04.13

Appraise a rent-producing property through lease-up and stabilization

The valuation course develops a corporate continuing value and a bank equity method. It does not derive stabilized property net operating income, separate tenant improvement spending from operating expenses, or value an unstabilized property before applying an exit capitalization rate.

  • Construct net operating income from rents, occupancy and operating costs on a stated convention.
  • Discount transitional property cash and a future sale, and stress the exit capitalization rate.

Public benchmarks

New York University Stern School of Business, United States

Valuing Private Companies and Divisions

Damodaran addresses private-company data, estimating risk without a quoted equity beta, the buyer's diversification, owner involvement and how the value of illiquidity depends on the business and the stake.

University of Oxford, Saïd Business School, United Kingdom

Note on Valuation and Mechanics of LBOs

Jenkinson and Stucke distinguish market peers from recent mergers or buyouts, use their earnings multiples to estimate private-company enterprise value and consider transaction sources, uses and value allocation.

Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and partner US banking agencies

Policy Statement on Prudent Commercial Real Estate Loan Accommodations and Workouts, Appendix 3 on valuation concepts

The income approach distinguishes stabilized net operating income suitable for direct capitalization from explicit discounting through a period of lease-up or distress, followed by a terminal property value.

New York University Stern School of Business, United States

Excess Returns and Terminal Value

The stable-growth discussion connects continuing earnings, the returns on reinvestment and the cash available for capitalization. It informs the comparison with a property whose current rental income is transitional.
