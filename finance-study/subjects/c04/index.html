Subject worked example

From operations to owners

Next year’s free cash flow to all investors is €6m. It grows 2% indefinitely. Capital costs 8%. Debt is €30m and excess cash is €5m.

Show the working

Operating value is 6/(0.08 − 0.02) = €100m. With no other claims, equity is 100 − 30 + 5 = €75m. A wider rate-growth gap lowers the valuation.

Course overview

What you will learn

Build an enterprise and equity valuation, reconcile them and reverse-engineer the expectations implied by a market price.

Theory and assumptions

Discounted cash flow, residual income and relative valuation. Growth creates value only when the return earned on additional investment justifies its cost.
