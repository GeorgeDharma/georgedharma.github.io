A lender, company and shareholder may all face the same price yet choose differently. Economics explains how constraints, preferences, private information and bargaining power influence those choices.

Two payments with different dates cannot be added meaningfully until they are translated to a common date. This principle underlies loans, bonds and business valuation.

Probability, Statistics and Inference

One profitable year could reflect skill, exposure to a rising market or chance. Statistics helps decide how much a limited set of observations can tell us.

Foundations

Financial Computing, Data and Reproducibility

A convincing calculation can be wrong because one invoice appeared twice or a price was recorded after the trade. Financial computing makes these errors visible and the analysis repeatable.

Open-Economy Macroeconomics and Monetary Systems

A country can import more than it exports for years. The question is how it pays, what it builds with the funding, and what happens if investors stop lending.

Core finance

Foreign Exchange and Multinational Financial Management

An Indonesian supplier may want rupiah while its French customer measures profit in euros. Prices, interest rates and the timing of payment connect the two markets.

A good project and a good financing plan answer different questions. Corporate finance studies investment, borrowing and payouts, including when these decisions affect one another.

A price is observable. A valuation makes explicit what a buyer must believe about future cash, growth and risk for that price to make sense.

Two risky assets can form a safer portfolio if their bad periods differ. Asset pricing asks which risks remain after diversification and why investors require compensation for them.

Core finance

Fixed Income and the Term Structure of Interest Rates

A bond can lose value while every promised payment is made. Fixed income separates the cash-flow schedule from movements in interest rates, credit risk and embedded options.

Core finance

Derivatives and Risk Transfer

A farmer, importer or lender may want to fix a future price. A derivative redistributes uncertainty, and its value depends on exactly what each party must pay in each state.

C08
Core finance

Banking and Financial Intermediation

Banks turn deposits that customers can withdraw into loans that may take years to repay. Studying that transformation explains both their economic value and their vulnerability.

Core finance

How does a company go public, who receives the cash, and when should an issuer change the price, resize or walk away?

S01
Advanced finance

Mergers and Acquisitions

Buying another company can create value through shared assets or destroy it through overpayment. The financial question is who receives the gains once the price, costs and risks of completion are counted.

S02
Advanced finance

Lending and Credit Analysis

A lender earns a limited contractual return and can lose much of the principal if repayment fails. Lending therefore begins with why the money is needed and where repayment will come from.

Advanced finance

Financial Due Diligence and Transaction Accounting

Two sellers can report the same profit while offering very different repeatable earnings and cash requirements. Due diligence tests the accounting evidence before a price becomes binding.

13 lessons
S04
Advanced finance

Corporate Distress and Restructuring

A firm can be worth more as a going concern than in a sale of its assets and still fail to meet next Friday’s payroll. A restructuring must solve both timing and allocation.

13 lessons
S05
Advanced finance

Private Equity, Venture Capital and Leveraged Finance

Private investors negotiate control, funding stages and exit rights alongside price. A quoted ownership percentage alone can tell little about who receives cash in a weak exit.

13 lessons
S06
Advanced finance

International Treasury and Trade Finance

A multinational can have enough cash overall yet lack the right currency in the entity that owes the supplier. Treasury coordinates cash, financing, hedges and payment terms over time.

S07
Advanced finance

Management Accounting, Planning and Control

A business needs information for choices that published accounts cannot settle, such as accepting a discounted order or funding an expansion. Management accounting studies which costs matter to each decision.

Advanced finance

Financial Risk Management and Regulation

A loss measure is meaningful only once the exposure, time horizon and tail probability are specified. Banks also need the capital and funding to survive losses outside their model.

Advanced finance

Market Microstructure, Trading and Settlement

A quoted price says little about the cost of buying a large position. Market microstructure studies how information, orders, dealers and market rules turn desired trades into transaction prices.

Advanced finance

Project, Development and Sustainable Finance

A power plant can be socially useful and still fail to repay a dollar loan if it earns only local currency. Project finance looks at the asset’s contracts and cash flows as the main sources of repayment.

Sovereign Debt and Emerging-Market Finance

A government can refinance in its own currency or owe foreign currency it cannot issue. Sovereign finance examines the interaction among debt arithmetic, policy credibility and market access.

S12
Advanced finance

Investment Management and Performance Evaluation

A portfolio can beat the stock market and still fail its owner if a pension payment falls due at the wrong time. Investment management begins with the investor’s liabilities, constraints and benchmark.
