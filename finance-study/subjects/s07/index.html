Subject worked example

A factory’s break-even volume

A product sells for €50, variable cost is €30 per unit and fixed cost is €100,000. Assume constant price and capacity within the range.

Show the working

Contribution is €20 per unit. Break-even is 100,000/20 = 5,000 units. At 6,000 units, operating profit is €20,000 before tax and financing.

Course overview

What you will learn

Build an operating budget, explain a performance variance and distinguish an economically useful measure from a misleading incentive.

Theory and assumptions

Cost–volume–profit analysis, relevant costs, responsibility centres and principal–agent incentives. A number suitable for external reporting can be unsuitable for an internal decision.
