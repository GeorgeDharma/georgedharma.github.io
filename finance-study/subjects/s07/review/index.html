New and expanded lessons

S07.11

Absorption costing, inventory and reported profit

S07.02 calculates contribution and S07.04 assigns support costs using activities. Neither case runs two income statements for the same sales and reconciles profit to fixed production overhead carried in unsold stock.

  • Reconcile absorption and variable-cost profit to inventory movement.
  • Calculate and expense underabsorbed fixed manufacturing overhead under a stated policy.

S07.12

Process costing and equivalent units of production

S07.04 prices activities and unused capacity, while F01.05 follows physical inventory. A production department also needs to cost units that have received all their material and only part of the labour and overhead needed to finish them.

  • Prepare a weighted-average process cost report from physical flows and completion estimates.
  • Reconcile both cost pools to completed units and closing work in progress.

S07.13

Target costing and lifetime product economics

S07.03 prices scarce capacity and S07.09 measures returns on invested capital. A new product needs a market-led allowable cost before engineering commits a design, including future warranty, disposal and up-front design expenditure.

  • Convert a target selling price and required margin into an allowable lifetime cost.
  • Use volume sensitivity and value engineering to test a proposed redesign.

Association of Chartered Certified Accountants

Management Accounting (MA/FMA), syllabus and study guide, September 2024 to August 2025

The learning outcomes require a profit reconciliation between marginal and absorption costing, cost assignment in processes, and explanation of target and life-cycle costing.

Southern Cross University, Australia

Modules include contribution and break-even, budget preparation, flexible budgets and variances, job and process costing, and ethical considerations.
