Professional workshop · C02

Hedge a French export contract without committing its unsigned extension

On 1 April a French equipment exporter needs a 30 September euro cash budget. The finance director wants a protected floor for the confirmed US order and a second floor if a distributor takes the optional extension. Currency moves and lost volume can occur together. Price the executable alternatives, compare them with a covered-parity reference, and submit a deal instruction that does not require an unconfirmed dollar delivery.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Group treasury analyst on a corporate FX dealing desk
What to submit
A treasury hedge ticket and a signed-style recommendation to the finance director, with a maturity-equivalent net-euro scenario grid, funding comparison, instrument quantities, premium budget and monitoring instructions.

From inputs to decision

01

Establish the commercial settlement positions

A hedge on USD 1.50m of gross sales would ignore the matching USD supplier bill. An unsigned extension has different delivery certainty from the existing contract.

  1. Link the two signed September claims. Net confirmed dollars available for a sale are 1.50 − 0.30 = USD 1.20m. At full award they increase to USD 1.80m.
  2. Before a contract is signed the permitted binding forward notional is at most USD 1.20m. The optional USD 0.60m needs a separate response if the full-award floor cannot otherwise be met.

Carry two volume cases, USD 1.20m and USD 1.80m. Each is tested at weak and strong dollar spots rather than forecasting a single sales result.

02

Check the reference forward and dealer execution sides

The desk should know whether the bank's quotation is plausible, without promising that it can fund at a reference rate. A short forward sells dollars and therefore uses the bank's bid.

  1. At reference terms the six-month forward is 0.900 × (1 + 0.02 × 0.5)/(1 + 0.04 × 0.5) = 0.891176 EUR/USD. The quote 0.889–0.894 brackets this reference.
  2. The executable firm hedge locks 1.20 × 0.889 = EUR 1.066800m. At 0.894 the bank sells dollars, which would be the wrong side for the exporter. Using the offer would overstate locked proceeds by EUR 6,000.

Use 0.889 on confirmed dollar sales in all policy comparisons and retain the reference figure only as an indicative control.

03

Price a money-market alternative using actual funding

Borrowing enough dollars to repay the loan with a fixed receipt replicates a sale of future dollars. It requires balance-sheet capacity and the company's actual borrowing rate.

  1. Borrow 1.20/(1 + 0.048 × 0.5) = USD 1.171875m today. Sell at the spot bid of 0.898 to receive EUR 1.05234375m. Deposit those euros for six months at 2% annual simple.
  2. September euros are 1.05234375 × 1.01 = EUR 1.06286719m. The dollar receipt repays USD 1.20m. The bank forward delivers EUR 1.066800m, an advantage of EUR 3,932.81 on the same maturity cash plus less balance-sheet usage under the case assumptions.

Select the forward for the USD 1.20m firm tranche. Recheck term sheets if the dollar invoice or counterparty terms change.

04

Compare covering the forecast with a full forward

A full USD 1.80m sale superficially meets the full-award floor. When only the committed sale arrives the extra USD 0.60m must be bought on the market to honor the contractual delivery.

  1. Full award fixes euro proceeds of 1.80 × 0.889 = EUR 1.600200m. Without the extension, net proceeds are 1.600200 − 0.60 × spot. At 0.80 the result is EUR 1.120200m and at 1.00 it falls to EUR 1.000200m.
  2. Forwarding only USD 1.20m avoids an excess delivery. With full award the unhedged extra receipts give 1.066800 + 0.60 × spot. At 0.80 this is EUR 1.546800m, below the required 1.58.

The full forward violates the delivery rule and misses the no-award cash floor at the strong endpoint. Confirmed-only forwarding misses the full-award weak-dollar floor.

05

Price the optional tranche on the same date

A cash-settled USD put pays euros when a dollar falls below the strike. The exporter owes no dollars under the option, even when the sales extension is cancelled. Its premium reduces cash under both volume cases.

  1. The upfront premium is 0.60m × EUR 0.012 = EUR 7,200. Its September equivalent is EUR 7,272 using the 1% half-year EUR return. At spot 0.80 the euro option payoff is 0.60m × (0.885 − 0.80) = EUR 51,000. At spot 1.00 its payoff is zero.
  2. With no award at weak spot, firm forward plus option yields 1.066800 + 0.051000 − 0.007272 = EUR 1.110528m. At strong spot no award gives EUR 1.059528m. With award, add USD 0.60m translated at the actual spot.

The euro option payoff is separate from the commercial order. Treating it as zero upon cancellation even though it is independently cash settled would misstate the weak-dollar no-award outcome.

06

Find the true floors over the spot interval

Checking just two endpoints can miss a nonlinear payoff. Inspect the kink at 0.885 and use the slope on either side before certifying the entire interval.

  1. No-award cash equals 1.066800 + 0.60 × max(0.885 − spot, 0) − 0.007272, in EUR millions. It falls with spot until 0.885, then stays at 1.059528. Its minimum exceeds 1.05 by EUR 9,528.
  2. Full-award cash adds 0.60 × spot. Up to strike, the drop in put value is exactly offset by better conversion of dollar sales. Minimum cash is 1.066800 + 0.60 × 0.885 − 0.007272 = EUR 1.590528m. Above strike it rises, reaching EUR 1.659528m at spot 1.00.

Both policy floors are met at every specified spot and the derivative program never requires physical delivery of uncertain dollars.

07

Issue the ticket and monitor the sales trigger

The desk needs amounts, directions and dates precise enough for independent trade confirmation. The sales team needs to understand what changes if the extension or original order moves.

  1. Book a 30 September deliverable forward to sell USD 1.20m at 0.889 for EUR 1.0668m. Buy a separate cash-settled European put on USD 0.60m at strike EUR 0.885 with the same maturity and EUR 7,200 premium due on trade date.
  2. Obtain dealer confirmation of the fixing, settlement calendar, quote convention and the exact cash-settlement formula. Review signed invoice and supplier dates weekly. A delay on the original USD receipt is a new liquidity event and the long put on the separate tranche does not fund an unmatched forward delivery.

Approve the layered policy. Reopen the instruction if the confirmed tranche, value date, option fixing or board floors change. Do not automatically enter a second forward when the extension is awarded without reassessing the existing option.

Completed document

Treasury hedge authorization · European export desk

Instruction

Sell USD 1.20m value 30 September at EUR 0.889 per dollar. Buy a 30 September cash-settled USD put for USD 0.60m at strike 0.885. Premium authorization is EUR 7,200 today. Settlement currency for the put is euro.

Rationale

The signed supplier invoice naturally covers USD 0.30m of the main order. Confirmed forwards alone fail the EUR 1.58m full-order floor at a weak dollar. Forwarding the entire unsigned amount introduces a prohibited delivery and a strong-dollar shortfall if the sale disappears.

Budget outcome

The selected structure secures minimum September-equivalent net euro cash of EUR 1.059528m without the extension and EUR 1.590528m with it. The firm's borrowing-based replication of the confirmed hedge delivers EUR 3,933 less than the executable forward.

Controls and exceptions

Treasury operations must independently match the fixing and value dates and confirm the dealer limits. Escalate any delay of the signed USD 1.50m receivable or USD 0.30m payable. Sales must report a cancellation or binding award so the desk can reassess the standalone option and any changed objectives.

Run a different case

Use the changed facts in each task. Enter numbers in the units shown, using a decimal point or comma and no thousands separators.

Task 1

At a 0.86 settlement spot with the extension awarded, calculate net maturity euro receipts under the selected policy, in EUR millions.

See the full solution

Firm forward gives 1.066800. Extra sales give 0.60 × 0.86 = 0.516. Put payoff is 0.60 × (0.885 − 0.86) = 0.015. Deduct 0.007272 premium equivalent to reach 1.590528. The weak-side floor holds because the put offsets the lower rate on optional sales.

Task 2

What is the maximum upfront option premium per USD that would satisfy both board floors with quantities and all other terms unchanged?

See the full solution

Without award the worst gross proceeds are 1.066800, leaving 0.016800m of maturity premium capacity. With award the worst gross proceeds are 1.597800, leaving 0.017800m. The no-award limit binds. Divide 0.016800 by 0.60 × 1.01 to get EUR 0.02772277 per USD.

Task 3

If actual extension receipts equal USD 0.30m and spot is 0.96, what is net September cash in EUR millions?

See the full solution

The signed tranche provides 1.066800. Half the optional order contributes 0.30 × 0.96 = 0.288. The put expires out of the money. After the 0.007272 premium equivalent, net cash is 1.347528.

Task 4

The original customer requests a one-month delay after the full structure is signed. What should appear in the revised treasury note?

List September cash shortfall, October receivable, unchanged September forward obligation and available dollar liquidity separately. Obtain the cost of a bridge or rolling the forward, assess counterparty limits and reconcile the put independently. The original maturity floor assumes matched September receipts.
