Subject worked example

Derive a forward quote

Spot is 1.10 US dollars per euro. One-year dollar and euro rates are 5% and 3%, with identical borrowing and lending rates in each currency.

Show the working

With dollars per euro, the one-year forward is 1.10 × 1.05/1.03 = 1.12136 dollars per euro. Both covered investment routes now produce the same dollars at maturity.

Course overview

What you will learn

Derive a forward exchange rate, distinguish three types of exposure and evaluate a foreign investment in a consistent currency.

Theory and assumptions

Purchasing power parity, covered and uncovered interest parity, and currency-consistent valuation. Covered parity is a replication argument under matched funding and trading assumptions.
