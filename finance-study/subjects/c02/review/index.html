New and expanded lessons

C02.11

Valuing a foreign project for its parent company

The course worked taxing and remitting subsidiary cash, and separately taught an adjusted present value financing concession. It had no case reconciling those pieces with an executable currency conversion and a direct parent-equity cash-flow check.

  • Reconcile foreign operating value to the cash that parent shareholders can receive.
  • Price a subsidised foreign loan against its market alternative, and check the resulting parent decision with direct cash flows.

C02.12

Pricing political-risk cover for an overseas investment

The existing remittance lesson models blocked cash and delayed transfers. It did not teach how to identify the covered event, value a partial and delayed insurance claim, and distinguish economic protection from a liquidity bridge.

  • Separate a government-imposed transfer block from a market loss or commercial default.
  • Calculate a delayed claim's value and identify the funding the parent still needs during the wait.

C02.13

FX settlement risk and payment versus payment

Existing currency lessons price a trade and hedge a future cash need. Securities clearing explains delivery against payment. There was no calculation of the interval of non-payment-versus-payment FX exposure or the difference between replacing a contract and losing its delivered principal.

  • Measure unsettled FX principal from the last guaranteed cancellation time to confirmed final receipt.
  • Compare replacement cost, principal exposure and liquidity need, then decide whether a settlement method meets a counterparty limit.

Public benchmarks

National Chengchi University, Taiwan

International Financial Management, Lecture 0 — Course Introduction

The course distinguishes the subsidiary and parent viewpoints in multinational investment and connects foreign financing to the assessment of risk.

MIT Sloan School of Management

Evaluating Foreign Projects — An Adjusted Present Value Approach

Donald Lessard's working paper separates cross-border investment cash flows from financing adjustments, including the economic benefit of concessionary borrowing.

Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency, World Bank Group

Investment Guarantee Guide

The guide distinguishes government restrictions on currency conversion or transfer from losses caused by market depreciation. This distinction motivates identifying insured states before valuing claims.

Basel Committee on Banking Supervision

Foreign exchange risks, chapter RMA20

The supervisory chapter identifies the time interval for principal exposure, distinguishes replacement and liquidity risk, and explains how payment versus payment limits principal risk.

Banque de France

Payments and market infrastructures in the digital era, chapter 9 — Systems operating on a payment versus payment basis

The central bank explains why exchange settlement across payment systems can put the full delivered principal at risk and describes the conditional settlement mechanism.
