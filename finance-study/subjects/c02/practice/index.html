Theory midterm · 10 marks

Borrow in one currency and cover the repayment

After lessons 1–5, allow 20 minutes. Explain the reasoning behind your answer.

Spot is 1.20 US dollars per euro. One-year deposits and loans both earn or cost 5% in dollars and 2% in euros. Quotes are executable and there are no costs or credit limits.

  1. Part A · 4 marks

    Derive the covered forward quote in dollars per euro.

  2. Part B · 3 marks

    A bank offers $1.26 per euro forward. Show a profitable covered transaction using €1 of spot principal.

  3. Part C · 3 marks

    Does the covered quote predict the future spot exactly?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the midterm marking guide

Part A · 4 marks

€1 becomes €1.02 in a year. Converting now gives $1.20, which becomes $1.26. Equating dollar payoffs gives 1.26/1.02 = $1.235294 per euro. Credit both paths, the common date and quote orientation.

Part B · 3 marks

Borrow $1.20, buy €1, invest it, and sell the €1.02 forward for $1.2852. Repay $1.26 to leave $0.0252. The forward locks the future conversion today.

Part C · 3 marks

No. It equates tradable financing paths using a locked conversion. A forecast of spot depends on beliefs and currency risk. Real funding spreads or credit limits can also narrow the apparent trading profit.

Problem 2

Why could covered parity calculation differ from an actual bank quote without offering a free profit?

Suggested academic marking scheme

Use these weights when assessing the complete subject. The four problem sets collect the written problems from individual lectures. You can use the 10-mark midterm and the 40-mark subject paper with the weights below.

Problem sets 25%
Four sets per standard subject. Show reasoning, calculations, units and checks. The two practice problems in each subject are worked samples for these sets.
Theory midterm 20%
After the first half of the subject. Explain or derive a model, identify assumptions and state what would change if one failed.
Seminar paper or laboratory 25%
During the second half. Apply the theory to a case, dataset or implementation. Address an alternative explanation and document how to reproduce the result.
Individual final examination 30%
At the end of the subject. Solve an unfamiliar problem, interpret the result and critique a claim. M&A and lending each include a 30-mark examination question with a marking guide.
