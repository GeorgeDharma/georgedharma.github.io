Chronological validation

Train on the past and test on the future. Add a gap when overlapping labels would otherwise share information between training and testing.

The starting point

Financial prediction runs forward. Train on data available up to an origin, tune on subsequent observations and reserve still later dates for final testing. An expanding window keeps the full history as the origin moves. A rolling window uses a fixed recent length and can respond differently to changing market conditions.

Each training example also has an outcome window. If a five-day forward return has not finished by the decision cutoff, its label is still unknown. Remove training examples whose outcome intervals reach into the validation or test period. Fit imputation, scaling, feature selection and model tuning within each training fold.

Theory and method

Nested rolling-origin evaluation with purged labels

Walk-forward validation · Temporal leakage

A signal researcher tunes a five-day strategy using monthly walk-forward folds. Precisely which training labels can be used at a validation start?

A model can train on past feature timestamps and still see future information if the outcome attached to a past feature finishes after the training cutoff. Nested rolling-origin evaluation uses inner historical folds for tuning and outer later folds for evaluation. Purging removes training samples whose label windows or release delays overlap forbidden information. A gap after a test block may also matter in designs that reuse later observations for training. The correct rule follows the actual information clock.

How the result is built

Eligible training origin t satisfies t + H + L ≤ C

t is a trading-day origin at close, H forward-label horizon in trading days, L additional vendor publication delay, and C the latest available training-data cutoff at close. The example assumes the outcome becomes available on that final close.

  1. Map every sample to its feature, execution, target-end and target-release dates. Keep only rows whose predictors and outcomes were both observable by C.
  2. For H=5, L=2 and C=80, t=73 is eligible and t=74 is not. The latter uses an outcome finalized at close 81.
  3. Run the same rule inside every inner tuning fold, fit all preprocessing there, freeze the selected procedure and generate the outer prediction without reusing its loss for tuning.

When the reasoning holds

  • The arithmetic assumes a known deterministic delay and a shared trading calendar. Security suspensions or vintage-specific revisions can require actual release records.
  • Overlapping outer forecast horizons can correlate evaluation losses even after training leakage is removed. Adjust the inference or use appropriately spaced origins.
First example

Index trading days by integers. A label at close t uses returns between close t and close t + 5. A model frozen at close 100 can use a label starting at 95, which ends at 100. A label starting at 96 ends at 101 and must wait.

Validation follows the information clock

A randomly shuffled split can put a firm’s future reports or a market regime’s later prices in the training set used to predict earlier dates. Time-ordered validation simulates what a researcher could have predicted at a specified date with the data available then.

An expanding window adds all newly available observations to the original training period. A rolling window discards sufficiently old observations. The first can improve precision in stable environments. The second can adapt more quickly after a break. Window selection is a modeling choice requiring validation.

Unfinished labels and overlapping outcomes

A signal observed at close t may predict return over (t, t + H]. At a cutoff C, its label is available only if t + H ≤ C and the required data have actually arrived. Removing recent labeled starts from training is sometimes called purging overlapping labels.

Adjacent validation labels can also share realized returns, creating dependence in evaluation scores. A chronological order alone does not make them independent. Use dependence-aware inference or nonoverlapping origins, and state what decision frequency the resulting evaluation represents.

Nest tuning inside the training history

A pipeline can leak through preprocessing, feature choice, hyperparameter tuning or early stopping. In each outer validation fold, train and tune any of those steps using inner history only. The outer fold evaluates the whole procedure. A genuinely untouched final period assesses the locked procedure once.

When predicting many stocks on the same date, split primarily by date for a future-market question. Randomly separating stocks while using later market outcomes from the same date across folds may evaluate another, much easier problem.

Method

Lay out a validation calendar

  1. Record each label interval

    Give every feature row a start time, the exact forecast end and the date the realized target became available.

  2. Filter at each origin

    Require both feature delivery and completed outcome before fitting. Fit scalers, imputers and model choices inside the permitted window.

  3. Preserve evaluation order

    Apply the trained procedure to future dates, keep predictions with timestamps and avoid revisiting the final sample for redesign.

Worked case

A rolling twenty-day target

A model forecasts twenty trading-day cumulative returns using information at close t. It will be frozen immediately after close 200 to make future forecasts. Assume closing data arrive without delay and that the return label ends at close t + 20.

Working

  1. The latest allowed training origin is 180 because its outcome ends at 200. An origin at 181 uses close 201, still unknown when the fit is frozen.
  2. A validation prediction issued at close 201 has an evaluation outcome ending at 221. Another issued at 202 ends at 222 and shares many individual daily returns with the first.
  3. Using both predictions can represent daily deployment. Their correlated losses should still receive suitable treatment if estimating the precision of the reported average.

Interpretation

If official data arrive one day later, origin 180’s label would be unavailable at the stated freeze. The release clock can be stricter than the calendar formula.

Draw the label horizon before splitting the data

If all features through day 9 are assigned to training, three labels bring later prices back across the boundary. Purge those rows or move the training cutoff. Fit transformations such as scaling and feature selection only on eligible training rows, freeze the procedure, and score the later block. Validation targets may overlap one another too, which changes performance uncertainty.

A common mistake

Create a time split after calculating normalization and feature selection on every date in the dataset.

How to correct it

Move all fitted transformations inside each training window. A validation date should influence evaluation of the trained procedure only after the prediction for that date was generated.

Quick check
With that same five-day label, what is the latest permissible training start if the model is frozen at close 120?

The full label must be known by day 120. The condition t + 5 ≤ 120 gives t ≤ 115. Later starting labels would use outcomes from the future.

Written practice · 8 marks

Independent exam problem

A five-day labeled outcome ending at close t + 5 is finalized by a vendor two trading days later. A model is fit immediately after all releases at close 60.

  1. Part A · 4 marks

    Find the latest usable training start and explain the two lags.

  2. Part B · 4 marks

    Why would holding out a random 20% of firms across all dates fail to answer whether the model forecasts next year’s market?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the worked marking guide

Part A 4 marks

Require t + 5 + 2 ≤ 60, so t ≤ 53. The five days span the target, and the extra two days represent data availability. An origin at 55 has finished economically but is not finalized.

Part B 4 marks

Training could use future market states relative to earlier held-out dates. A date-based outer split with correctly timestamped features and labels is needed to mimic the future-year task. Firm holdouts address a different generalization dimension.

Interview practice

Explain it aloud

A signal researcher tunes a five-day strategy using monthly walk-forward folds. Precisely which training labels can be used at a validation start?

Compare with a spoken answer

I would require the training label’s final observation and vendor delivery to precede the training cutoff, then repeat that rule independently inside the hyperparameter search. Under these five-day labels and two-day delays, the last usable start at close 80 is day 73. Keeping chronologically sorted rows without checking their outcome windows would still leak later prices.

Interviewer’s follow-up

Is a separate embargo after the outer test always necessary in a strictly expanding training window?

Compare with the follow-up answer

If training uses only dates strictly before the test and label availability is already enforced, later post-test observations never enter that fit. An extra embargo can serve other dependence or research-design goals, but its necessity depends on which intervals can reach the training set.
