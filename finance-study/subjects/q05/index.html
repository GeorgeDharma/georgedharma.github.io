Subject worked example

What turnover costs

A simulated portfolio earns 1.2% gross this month. Executed buys plus sells equal 80% of starting portfolio value. One-way trading cost is 0.15% per euro traded.

Show the working

Cost is 0.80 × 0.15% = 0.12% of starting value. Net return is 1.08%, before other fees. A one-way or half-turnover convention would require a different input.

Course overview

What you will learn

Design a dated research experiment, compare a machine-learning model with a simple baseline and construct a feasible portfolio after trading costs.

Theory and assumptions

Empirical asset pricing, statistical learning, regularization and constrained portfolio optimization. An independent test period and feasible implementation are essential to interpreting measured performance.
