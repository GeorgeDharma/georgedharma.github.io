New and expanded lessons

C06.11

Mortgage pass-throughs and prepayment speeds

The structured-debt lesson identifies mortgage refinancing and sequential principal. Its examples take available principal as given. This chapter builds that principal from scheduled amortization and a conditional pool prepayment rate, then reconciles the investor remittance.

  • Convert annualized prepayment speed into a monthly conditional rate.
  • Reconcile interest, scheduled principal, prepayment and the remaining pool factor.

C06.12

Asset-backed securities, reserve accounts and loss triggers

The existing waterfall orders losses or principal among claims. It does not reconcile borrower collections, noncash write-offs, excess interest, a reserve release and a delinquency-related switch inside an operating receivables trust.

  • Separate asset losses, cash collection and credit protection.
  • Apply a performance trigger and reconcile ending receivables, notes and overcollateralization.

C06.13

Inflation-linked bonds, indexed settlement and redemption floors

The curve course and sovereign subject discuss nominal versus real yields and inflation risk. A learner had yet to turn a quoted real bond price, real accrued interest and a published index ratio into settlement cash or test a maturity floor.

  • Reconcile an indexed bond’s quoted price with cash due at settlement.
  • Distinguish indexed coupons and a contractual principal floor from the investor’s purchase cost.

C06.14

Repo and reverse repo, haircuts and daily margin

The credit-spread lesson mentions repo availability, and the bank workshop applies a haircut to secured funding capacity. Neither reconciles a securities financing trade from its opening cash price through daily rate accrual, collateral revaluation and repurchase.

  • Build the opening and closing cash and securities movements for both repo parties.
  • Distinguish repo interest, the initial haircut, and a subsequent call for cash or additional securities.
