Duration

Modified duration approximates the percentage price change for a small yield move. State the sign, scale and assumed shape of the move.

The starting point

If rates rise, the present value of fixed future payments generally falls. Modified duration summarizes how sensitive the price is to a small yield change. Multiply the duration by the change expressed as a decimal and reverse the sign to estimate the proportional price change.

Macaulay duration uses the present-value-weighted timing of the cash flows. For an annual compound yield, dividing Macaulay duration by one plus that yield gives modified duration. Applying a single duration to a whole portfolio generally assumes a parallel rate shift. Embedded options or different moves at different maturities can change the response.

Theory and method

Fisher–Weil duration

Parallel spot-curve duration · Dollar duration

Which duration should you use if a non-flat spot curve rather than a single yield prices the bond?

An insurer values a €10 receipt in one year and €110 in three years using different discount factors. A common small increase in continuously compounded spot rates changes each date’s present value by an amount proportional to the time until that payment. Fisher–Weil duration aggregates those dated sensitivities on the actual curve. It allows a non-flat starting curve while still modeling a parallel shock. A change confined to only one segment requires key-rate analysis.

How the result is built

Dꜰᴡ = ΣtCₜdₜ/P, and ΔP ≈ −PDꜰᴡΔs

Cₜ is the euro cash flow after t years, dₜ its current discount factor, P the full euro present price, Dꜰᴡ the duration in years, and Δs the change in continuously compounded spot rates in decimal per year.

  1. Apply the same shift Δs to each continuously compounded spot rate. Each current present cash flow is then multiplied by exp(−tΔs).
  2. The first derivative of each term at zero shift is −tCₜdₜ. Sum the derivatives and divide the negative by price to get the weighted duration.
  3. With d₁ = 0.98 and d₃ = 0.87, price is €9.80 + €95.70 = €105.50. The numerator is €9.80 + 3 × €95.70 = €296.90 years-euros, giving 2.8142 years.

When the reasoning holds

  • The modeled cash amounts are fixed. Early redemption, defaults or future coupon resets can require a full model-based repricing.
  • The shift is parallel in continuously compounded spot rates. A common shock to quoted par yields or a twist can produce different present-value changes.

Terms in this lesson
Duration
A measure used to estimate how strongly a bond price responds to a small interest-rate change.
Present value (PV)
An amount at a future date expressed in today’s money using a rate suited to its timing and risk.
Cash flow
Money actually entering or leaving an account over a period.
Option
A contract giving the buyer a right to buy or sell on stated terms. The buyer pays a premium for the right.
Discount rate
Rate used to express a future payment in today’s money, reflecting time and the relevant risk.
Bond
A loan that can be bought and sold. Its terms say when the borrower must pay interest and repay the amount borrowed.
How to read the notation
Basis points and percentage points
A rate rising from 4% to 5% rises by one percentage point, or 100 basis points. Its increase relative to the original rate is (5 − 4) ÷ 4 = 25%.
Powers · (1 + r)²
The small 2 means multiply the factor by itself. Two years of 5% compound growth turn €100 into €100 × 1.05 × 1.05 = €110.25. Plain text also writes the power as ^2.
Approximately equal · ≈
The two numbers match to the precision shown. €100 ÷ 3 ≈ €33.33. Keep more digits while calculating, then round the final answer to the precision requested.

All notation examples

First example

A portfolio worth €2m has modified duration 4.5. A 0.30 percentage-point yield rise gives an approximate move of −4.5 × 0.003 = −1.35%, a loss of about €27,000.

Derive duration from a cash-flow schedule

A payment a long way in the future loses more present value than an equally valuable near payment when the discount rate rises. Macaulay duration averages payment dates using the share of the bond’s present value contributed by each cash flow. For a conventional positive stream it lies between the earliest and latest payment dates.

Modified duration turns the timing measure into a price sensitivity. With annual compounding divide Macaulay duration by 1 + y. For m compounding periods per year use 1 + y/m when y is the matching nominal yield. State how time is measured and keep yield changes in decimals.

Convert percentage sensitivity into money

A duration of five means that a yield increase of one percentage point implies an approximate 5% price decrease, locally. DV01 or PV01 often denotes the magnitude of the currency movement for one basis point, 0.0001 in decimal yield. Some desks use signed figures. Explicitly state the direction.

For a portfolio, combine money sensitivities or market-value-weighted durations on a consistent curve. Face-value weights can mislead when holdings trade away from par. Liabilities move too, so a bond loss may be offset by a decline in the value of a matching obligation.

Know which yield is moving

Modified duration from a single yield treats the entire schedule as fixed while that yield moves. If different parts of the curve move differently, the bond’s exposure needs date-specific sensitivities. For callable instruments the expected cash flows can themselves change with rates.

Duration is a first-order approximation. Large changes introduce curvature and even short holding periods involve accrued coupon and time passage. To test a hedge, distinguish the price impact of a rate shock from other return components.

Method

Compute and check local risk

  1. Value

    Discount each scheduled payment, add present values, and divide each contribution by total price to form weights summing to one.

  2. Differentiate

    Compute Macaulay and then modified duration under the chosen quote. Convert it to cash change for the actual holding.

  3. Reprice

    Apply the rate shock directly to the cash flows and compare the exact price with the first-order estimate.

Worked case

Coupon timing reduces rate sensitivity

A two-year €100 bond pays €5 after one year and €105 after two, at a 5% annual compound yield on a coupon date. A second investment is a two-year zero.

Working

  1. The coupon bond price is €5/1.05 + €105/1.05² = €100. Present-value weights are approximately 0.047619 and 0.952381, giving Macaulay duration 1.952381 years.
  2. Modified duration is 1.952381/1.05 ≈ 1.85941. For €2m market value a one-basis-point rise produces an approximate loss of €2m × 1.85941 × 0.0001 ≈ €372.
  3. The two-year zero has Macaulay duration 2 and modified duration about 1.90476. Per euro of starting value it loses slightly more on the same rise because no part is received early.

Interpretation

The calculation holds the yield convention and cash schedule constant. A parallel curve change and an issue-specific credit-spread move can generate numerically similar local effects while representing different risks.

At the board

See the curvature that duration misses

A bond pays a single 100 in two years. Its price at annual effective yield y is 100/(1+y)². At 5% it is 90.7029 and its modified duration is 2/1.05 = 1.9048 years. The duration estimate holds the slope fixed.

Annual yieldExact priceDuration-only estimate from 5%
4%92.455690.7029 × (1 + 1.9048 × 0.01) = 92.4306
5%90.702990.7029
6%88.999690.7029 × (1 − 1.9048 × 0.01) = 88.9753

The exact prices lie above the straight-line estimate on both sides. For this ordinary fixed payment, price is convex in yield. A one-point fall gains about 1.7527 price units, while a one-point rise loses about 1.7033. Quoting “duration 1.9” without the initial price and the yield change would not give a cash loss.

A common mistake

Multiplying duration by 50 to apply a 50-basis-point shock.

How to correct it

Write 50 basis points as 0.005. A separate multiplication by market value converts the resulting proportional change into currency.

Quick check
For a €500,000 bond position with modified duration 3, what is the approximate effect of a 0.20 percentage-point yield fall?

The estimated price move is −3 × (−0.002) = +0.006, or +0.6%. Multiplying by €500,000 gives €3,000.

Written practice · 5 marks

Independent exam problem

A position worth €4m has modified duration 3.2. A second position worth €1m has duration 7.2 under the same convention.

  1. Part A · 3 marks

    Find portfolio duration and its approximate loss on a parallel 15-basis-point rise.

  2. Part B · 2 marks

    If a €30,000 loss is actually observed, has this proven that rates rose exactly 15 basis points?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the worked marking guide

Part A 3 marks

Market-value weights are 0.8 and 0.2. Duration is 0.8 × 3.2 + 0.2 × 7.2 = 4.0. Estimated loss is €5m × 4.0 × 0.0015 = €30,000.

Part B 2 marks

No. Credit spreads, curve twists, carry, trading and convexity can contribute. The calculation maps an assumed isolated shock to an approximate result, not a unique explanation of a portfolio change.

Interview practice

Explain it aloud

Which duration should you use if a non-flat spot curve rather than a single yield prices the bond?

Saved on this device as you type.
Compare with a spoken answer

I would use Fisher–Weil duration for a specified parallel change in the spot curve. Here the discounted cash-weighted maturity is 2.814 years and the euro loss for one basis point is about €0.02969 on €105.50 market value. I would state the compounding convention and derive the change from the starting curve. For an isolated three-year move I would use that date’s own sensitivity.

Interviewer’s follow-up

The one-year spot rate rises 15 basis points while the three-year spot rate falls 5 basis points. What is the local euro gain?

Compare with the follow-up answer

Allocate the shock to each cash flow. The one-year effect is −€0.00098 × 15 = −€0.01470. The three-year effect is +€0.02871 × 5 = +€0.14355. The approximate net gain is €0.12885 per bond. The longer exposure dominates despite its smaller rate shock.
