Fixed Income and the Term Structure of Interest Rates

A bond can lose value while every promised payment is made. Fixed income separates the cash-flow schedule from movements in interest rates, credit risk and embedded options.

Subject worked example

Price and duration

A two-year bond pays €5 in year one and €105 in year two. At a flat annual yield of 5%, it trades at €100.

Show the working

At 6%, price is 5/1.06 + 105/1.06² = €98.17. The promised cash is unchanged. Investors now discount those amounts more heavily.

Course overview

What you will learn

Price bonds from discount factors, estimate interest-rate sensitivity and separate a credit-spread move from a benchmark-rate move.

Theory and assumptions

No-arbitrage discount curves, duration and convexity, and term-structure theories. A single yield summarizes one bond and generally cannot price every dated cash flow.
