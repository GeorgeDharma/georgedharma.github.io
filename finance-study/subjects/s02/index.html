S02Advanced finance

Lending and Credit Analysis

A lender earns a limited contractual return and can lose much of the principal if repayment fails. Lending therefore begins with why the money is needed and where repayment will come from.

Subject worked example

Repayment before collateral

An exporter has €1.50m cash available for annual debt service, €0.30m interest and €0.90m principal due. Assume all amounts refer to the same year.

Show the working

Debt service is €1.20m. Coverage is 1.50/1.20 = 1.25× with €0.30m cash headroom. A 25% fall in available cash leaves €1.125m and a €0.075m cash shortfall.

Course overview

What you will learn

Underwrite a borrower, distinguish cash-flow and collateral support, price expected loss and design terms that address the source of risk.

Theory and assumptions

Asymmetric information, delegated monitoring, credit rationing and debt contracts. Higher interest rates can change both the pool of borrowers and the risks that funded borrowers choose.
