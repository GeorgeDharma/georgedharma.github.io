Case 1 of 4 · 10 marks

A first facility for a seasonal exporter

A supplier has €1.2m EBITDA, pays €180,000 tax, spends €200,000 on upkeep and ties up €220,000 more operating working capital. Existing annual debt service is €260,000. The lender requires total coverage of 1.25. The proposed terminal-year interest and principal together add €200,000. A separate new €90,000 peak in invoices will be collected three months after the first payment.

Case 2 of 4 · 10 marks

A borrowing base and a quoted spread

A one-year line is committed for €600,000. Receivables total €800,000, with €170,000 ineligible and a further €50,000 excluded by the concentration cap. Advance rate is 75%, and €380,000 has been drawn. Separately price a €100 tranche whose terminal funding and cost hurdle is €103, PD is 4%, and recovery is €30 at year-end.

Case 3 of 4 · 10 marks

What the loan score can establish

Two risk bands contain 1,000 and 250 one-year loans. Predicted PDs were 2% and 8%. Actual defaults were 30 and 30. Each performing loan brings €600 net contribution, each default loses €5,400. A rate increase could alter which borrowers apply. The two bands cover only approved customers.

Case 4 of 4 · 10 marks

Workout of a foreign-currency facility

The lender can collect US$240,000 in liquidation today after enforcing priority. A restructuring requires US$15,000 new cash now. In one year it returns US$340,000 with probability 70%, otherwise US$140,000. Discount receipts by 10%. Local rules in the case allow a transfer of at most US$260,000 in either branch while a restriction is active.

Case 1 · 10 marks

A first facility for a seasonal exporter

Part A 4 marks

Calculate available cash, maximum new annual service and coverage on the requested structure.

Available cash is €600,000. Total service ceiling is €480,000, leaving €220,000 for new service. Requested coverage is €600,000/€460,000 ≈ 1.30, just above the floor. Award one mark for each result and one for interpreting compliance.

Part B 3 marks

Assume a permanent €120,000 drop in recurring EBITDA with the other annual inputs unchanged. Resize service and explain whether the same facility fits.

Cash becomes €480,000. Total permitted service becomes €384,000, leaving €124,000 for the new facility. The original adds €200,000 and therefore misses the floor in this downside. Award one mark per numerical step and conclusion.

Part C 3 marks

Explain how to investigate the €90,000 peak without counting it twice.

Reconcile it with the €220,000 annual change to determine whether it is an additional temporary swing or already included. Build a dated cash forecast through the first payment. Verify invoices and collection dates, then test a seasonal line repaid on collection under applicable availability terms. Award one mark for reconciliation, timing and financing logic respectively.

Case 2 · 10 marks

A borrowing base and a quoted spread

Part A 3 marks

Find the allowed line, current headroom and remaining headroom if another €80,000 becomes ineligible.

Eligible base = €580,000, supporting €435,000 below the commitment. Headroom is €55,000. After another €80,000 exclusion, the new line is €375,000 and existing drawings exceed it by €5,000. Award one mark per step.

Part B 4 marks

Derive the tranche’s contractual break-even rate and expected loss of principal assuming EAD equals €100.

Solve 0.96 × payment + 0.04 × €30 = €103. Payment is €106.0417 and contractual rate about 6.04%. Expected principal loss is 0.04 × (€100 − €30) = €2.80. Award one mark each for branch equation, payment, rate and loss.

Part C 3 marks

Give one action on the overadvance, one risk in assuming the quoted recovery, and one implication for pricing.

Verify disputed invoices and inspect contractual cures or require a documented paydown. Recovery can deteriorate when the same customer weakens multiple invoices. A lower recovery raises the required price and may render the facility unaffordable. Award a mark per distinct point.

Case 3 · 10 marks

What the loan score can establish

Part A 4 marks

Calculate observed rates and explain ranking and calibration separately.

Observed rates are 3% and 12%. The higher-risk band still defaults more often. Both observed rates exceed forecasts, so those probabilities understate this cohort’s realized frequency. Award one mark for each result and distinction, up to four.

Part B 3 marks

Find the break-even PD and assess both bands using realized rates as provisional forecasts.

Set (1 − p) × €600 − p × €5,400 = 0, so p = 10%. The 3% band has positive expected contribution of €420 per loan. The 12% band has negative contribution of €120. Award one mark for threshold, one for each band and interpretation.

Part C 3 marks

Why is an increase in the high-band rate not a guaranteed solution? Outline a test.

The rate can deter safer customers or alter project incentives. Observe applicant composition and take-up before and after offers, including declines, while accounting for economic conditions. Check subsequent outcomes by fixed offer cohort and time. Award one mark for mechanism, evidence and comparison design.

Case 4 · 10 marks

Workout of a foreign-currency facility

Part A 3 marks

Compare restructuring with liquidation without the transfer restriction.

Weighted proceeds are US$280,000. Discounted net value is US$280,000/1.10 − US$15,000 ≈ US$239,545. Liquidation is higher by about US$455. Award one mark each for expected receipts, discount and net comparison.

Part B 4 marks

Recalculate assuming the restriction applies throughout, with excess receipts inaccessible to this lender.

The success branch is capped at US$260,000 and the other remains US$140,000. Expected receipts fall to US$224,000. Present net value is about US$188,636, so liquidation is better by about US$51,364. Award one mark each for cash cap, weighting, value and conclusion.

Part C 3 marks

State three distinct items of evidence before the credit committee decides.

Obtain evidence that the liquidation priority and timing are enforceable, verify transfer terms and access to any offshore proceeds, and test the forecast underpinning the 70% turnaround probability with orders or signed funding. Award one mark for each distinct evidence request tied to the decision.
