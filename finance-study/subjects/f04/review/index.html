New and expanded lessons

F04.11

Is one approval or response rate really higher?

F04 gives intervals for means and a general testing framework. This chapter works the binary-outcome case with different denominators, a pooled equality test, a separate uncertainty interval and an explicit percentage-point interpretation.

  • Test equality of two independent population rates under the stated normal approximation.
  • Give an interval for the absolute rate difference and recognize when the design breaks its assumptions.

F04.12

Test an intervention by replaying its assignment

F04 discusses experimental design and hypothesis tests. The new work builds the complete null distribution from the assignment rule, including ties and a paired redesign, so learners can justify a small-sample p-value without a normal approximation.

  • Enumerate the assignments permitted by a randomized design under a sharp null.
  • Choose a direction and calculate an exact tail probability, preserving pairing when appropriate.

F04.13

Use the observed cases to estimate sampling uncertainty

F04 derives intervals analytically and Q05 uses bootstrap samples to train trees. Here the resampling distribution is itself the object of study. Learners construct it, read a percentile interval and repair the design for clustered or paired observations.

  • Build bootstrap replicates from units sampled with replacement and distinguish a replicate from the original estimate.
  • Read an empirical percentile interval and identify cases where resampling individual rows is inadequate.
