Subject worked example

What a warning means

Of 1,000 loans, 20 default. A warning flags 15 of the defaults and 85 performing loans.

Show the working

The warning catches 15/20 = 75% of defaults, yet only 15/(15 + 85) = 15% of flagged loans default. The two probabilities condition on different groups.

Course overview

What you will learn

Summarize a distribution, update a probability, estimate uncertainty and distinguish an association in a sample from a causal claim.

Theory and assumptions

Conditional probability, sampling theory and least squares. Sampling intervals and tests rely on assumptions about how observations were generated and how strongly they depend on one another.
