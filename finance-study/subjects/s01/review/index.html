New and expanded lessons

S01.13

Pricing a share-exchange collar before a merger closes

The exchange-ratio lesson shows how each side sets a bargaining bound, and mentions price collars. The buyer model assumes a specified issuance. This chapter computes the changing exchange ratio between signing and closing.

  • Calculate seller proceeds and buyer share issuance throughout a collar.
  • Identify which shareholders bear a price move in each interval.

S01.14

Selling, spinning off or partially listing a division

The control and integration units ask whether joining businesses creates value. A parent board also needs a decision model for which separation route leaves existing owners better off and how much cash the parent receives.

  • Reconcile parent cash and shareholder value under three separation structures.
  • Calculate the minimum sale offer that matches a spin-off after leakage.

S01.15

Building a precedent-transaction valuation for a buyer’s board

The valuation subject teaches listed peer multiples and M&A measures changes in control value. This chapter reconstructs acquisition enterprise values from announced terms, screens the transaction sample and translates a control-deal range into a price per target share.

  • Construct consistently measured acquisition multiples from publicly stated terms.
  • Apply a screened range to the target and compare the result with its unaffected price and buyer-specific limit.

Public benchmarks

MIT Sloan School of Management

15.S22 Mergers and Acquisitions, IAP 2025

The syllabus connects earnings accretion, sources of merger value, forms of payment and announcement reactions to the distribution of value. It expressly includes the use of collars, caps and floors in deal design.

New York University Stern School of Business

Beyond Cash Dividends: Buybacks, Spin Offs and Divestitures

Aswath Damodaran distinguishes selling an operation, distributing its shares and selling a minority equity interest. He examines cash raised, retained control, economic motivation and evidence on value after separation.

New York University Tandon School of Engineering

FIN-UY 4903 Investment Banking and Private Equity, classes 7 and 8

The investment banking syllabus teaches selection and tabulation of precedent deals, benchmarking and implied valuation, followed by merger models, proxy statements and the sale process.

University of Notre Dame Mendoza College of Business

FIN 70400/70401 Mergers & Acquisitions

The course explicitly distinguishes precedent deals, trading comparables and discounted cash flow as target valuation methods. It also asks students to calculate synergies and the most a particular buyer should be willing to pay.

National University of Singapore Business School

FIN4714 Valuation and Mergers & Acquisitions

The syllabus links corporate-control decisions to the division of value through deal structure. Its acquisition and synergy readings follow a valuation block, providing a benchmark for comparing a proposed structure with standalone value.
