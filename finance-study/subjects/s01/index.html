S01Advanced finance

Mergers and Acquisitions

Buying another company can create value through shared assets or destroy it through overpayment. The financial question is who receives the gains once the price, costs and risks of completion are counted.

Subject worked example

A maximum offer

The target is worth €100m as a standalone equity investment. Gross synergy has present value €32m and integration costs have present value €8m. The offer is €115m.

Show the working

Net synergy is €24m. The premium over standalone value is €15m. Buyer gain is €9m and seller gain is €15m. The zero-gain ceiling is €124m if the buyer requires no additional buffer.

Course overview

What you will learn

Derive a maximum price, compare cash with shares, interpret announcement returns and defend a deal decision under explicit alternative explanations.

Theory and assumptions

Corporate control, agency, synergy and bargaining. On a consistent equity basis, the buyer’s gain equals net synergy less the premium paid over the target’s standalone value.
