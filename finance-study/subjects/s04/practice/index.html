Theory midterm · 10 marks

Can a solvent factory miss Friday’s payroll?

After lessons 1–5, allow 20 minutes. Explain the reasoning behind your answer.

A factory has 15 of unrestricted cash. Payroll of 25 is due Friday. A committed customer pays 30 next Tuesday. Equipment could sell for 80 net but would take six weeks. Total liabilities, including payroll, are 70. Ignore other flows.

  1. Part A · 4 marks

    Separate Friday liquidity from estimated asset coverage.

  2. Part B · 3 marks

    What is the smallest bridge through Friday and how could it be repaid?

  3. Part C · 3 marks

    What evidence might overturn the solvency or liquidity calculation?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the midterm marking guide

Part A · 4 marks

On Friday the business is short 25 − 15 = 10. On the stated valuations, assets are 15 + 30 + 80 = 125 against 70 of liabilities, for a surplus of 55. Credit the cash deficit, timing, asset total and surplus.

Part B · 3 marks

A facility of 10 available by Friday closes the gap. Tuesday’s 30 can repay its principal and leave 20 before interest and other payments. Verify the collection and the lender’s funding date.

Part C · 3 marks

Test the forced-sale price and delay, the customer’s ability to pay and any restrictions on cash or priority claims. Lower asset recoveries affect solvency, while a later collection can extend the funding gap.

Problem 1

Weekly opening cash is €1.2m, collections €0.8m and scheduled payments €2.4m. How much funding is needed for a €0.3m minimum close?

Show the worked solution

Closing cash before funding is −€0.4m. A €0.7m facility draw brings closing cash to €0.3m.

Assessment criteria
  • Apply the right theory and state the assumptions.
  • Show each step with consistent units, dates and currency.
  • Interpret the result, test an alternative and explain what could change the decision.
Suggested academic marking scheme

Use these weights when assessing the complete subject. The four problem sets collect the written problems from individual lectures. You can use the 10-mark midterm and the 40-mark subject paper with the weights below.

Problem sets 25%
Four sets per standard subject. Show reasoning, calculations, units and checks. The two practice problems in each subject are worked samples for these sets.
Theory midterm 20%
After the first half of the subject. Explain or derive a model, identify assumptions and state what would change if one failed.
Seminar paper or laboratory 25%
During the second half. Apply the theory to a case, dataset or implementation. Address an alternative explanation and document how to reproduce the result.
Individual final examination 30%
At the end of the subject. Solve an unfamiliar problem, interpret the result and critique a claim. M&A and lending each include a 30-mark examination question with a marking guide.
