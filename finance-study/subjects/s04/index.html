Corporate Distress and Restructuring

A firm can be worth more as a going concern than in a sale of its assets and still fail to meet next Friday’s payroll. A restructuring must solve both timing and allocation.

Subject worked example

Divide the available proceeds

A sale produces €70m after costs. Secured debt of €50m ranks ahead of unsecured debt of €40m. Assume no other claims.

Show the working

Secured creditors receive €50m, or 100%. Unsecured creditors receive €20m, or 50%. Equity receives zero. A €10m decline in proceeds reduces unsecured recovery to 25%.

Course overview

What you will learn

Diagnose distress, build a short-term liquidity forecast and compare creditor recoveries under two coherent options.

Theory and assumptions

Debt overhang, creditor coordination, priority and going-concern surplus. A viable rescue can fail if the party providing new money cannot capture enough of the additional value.
