Subject worked example

An unpaid sale

A shop pays €60 cash for stock, then delivers all of it to a customer for €100 on credit. Ignore tax and other costs.

Show the working

Profit is €40. Cash has fallen €60. The customer owes €100. Starting from profit, €40 minus the €100 increase in receivables gives operating cash flow of −€60.

Course overview

What you will learn

Build the three statements from transactions, reconcile profit to cash and identify one policy that weakens a comparison between companies.

Theory and assumptions

Accrual accounting and the accounting equation. Assets equal liabilities plus equity. Estimates of useful life, collectability and timing influence reported earnings.
