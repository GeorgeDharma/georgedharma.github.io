Green proceeds, sustainability targets and financial consequences

Theory and terminology

Green bonds and sustainability-linked debt

use of proceeds · sustainability performance target · coupon step-up

What you should be able to explain

  • Reconcile an eligible asset register to net green proceeds and disclose unallocated funds.
  • Test a sustainability-linked target and calculate the amount and timing of a contractual coupon change.

Follow the money for a green use-of-proceeds instrument

A green bond can be a general obligation of the issuer whose net proceeds are allocated to eligible environmental projects. A framework sets the types of projects that qualify, how they are selected, how amounts are tracked and how allocation and impact are reported. The ICMA principles cited here are voluntary market guidelines.

Project eligibility and payment evidence are different checks. Verify invoices, relevant dates, refinancing look-back and the proportion already financed by another allocation. A project’s gross budget is not automatically available to absorb this bond. Until eligible allocation occurs, disclose the unallocated balance and its stated temporary management.

Test the promised performance for linked debt

A sustainability-linked bond can fund general corporate purposes while its financial characteristics depend on a published performance target. A key performance indicator identifies the measured activity. A sustainability performance target sets the required level and observation date. Materiality, ambition, verification and the consequences of missing a target matter.

Read the baseline, units, organizational boundary and adjustment rules. A falling intensity score, such as tonnes per unit of production, can coexist with rising absolute emissions if production expands. Failure to deliver verified data is governed by the contract’s fallback wording.

Separate claims, pricing and impacts

An allocation report can state how much qualifying expenditure was financed. An environmental impact estimate needs a credible counterfactual and measurement boundary. An investor can fund an eligible project while the issuer’s other operations deteriorate.

A coupon step-up increases future debt service under the contract. Its financial size can be modest relative to the business. A missed target may also matter to an investor’s mandate even when all debt payments continue. Distinguish the economic incentives and the actual contractual credit event, if any.

Method

Build the result

Unallocated proceeds = net proceeds − eligible unassigned allocations. Additional coupon cash = outstanding principal × step-up rate × applicable period fraction.

Net proceeds and allocations are in a common currency. Eligible unassigned allocations exclude ineligible, out-of-window and previously allocated expenditure. The step-up rate is a decimal annual increase. The period fraction is the part of a year covered by the changed rate.

  1. Reconcile bond cash received, issuance deductions and the eligible asset register. Check for overlaps with previous allocations.
  2. For a separate linked obligation, calculate the specified KPI at its observation date against the target using the signed measurement and verification rules.
  3. Apply the contractual rate increase only to affected coupon dates and outstanding principal. Discount these payments when a present value is requested.
Worked case

A utility files two different financing reports

A green issue receives €100m before €2m expenses. Its framework permits €30m solar invoices, €35m transit investment of which €10m already backs another allocation, and €20m retrofit invoices of which €5m precedes the permitted window. €12m ordinary operations is ineligible. A separate €80m linked bond measures annual emissions intensity. If verified intensity exceeds 80 tonnes per GWh, the next two annual coupons rise 25 basis points.

Working

  1. Eligible allocations are €30m + (€35m − €10m) + (€20m − €5m) = €70m. Net proceeds are €98m and €28m remains unallocated.
  2. Verified emissions of 85,000 tonnes on 1,000 GWh give intensity of 85 tonnes/GWh. It exceeds the threshold of 80, triggering the linked bond’s contractual coupon change.
  3. A basis point is 0.01 percentage point. A 25 basis point step is 0.0025 of principal. Extra cash is €80m × 0.0025 = €0.20m per full annual coupon, or €0.40m over two years.
  4. At the observation date, if the first extra coupon is in one year and the second in two, their value at 5% is 0.20/1.05 + 0.20/1.05² = €0.37188m.

Decision and interpretation

Publish the €28m unallocated balance and its planned treatment alongside a separate target verification and payment schedule. Eligible green expenditures cannot cure the missed target under the stated linked contract.

Quick check
A linked note of €40m steps up by 50 basis points for exactly six months after a target failure. What extra coupon cash does this clause create?

The step is 0.005 per year. €40m × 0.005 × 0.5 = €100,000 for half a year.

Written practice

Independent exam problem

A green project issue yields €59m net. €24m fully eligible expenditure and €20m other eligible spending are documented, but €6m of the second group is already allocated. Separately, a €60m linked bond misses a verified target and its terms increase one full annual coupon by 30 basis points. What amounts belong in each report?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the worked solution
  1. Eligible allocation is €24m + (€20m − €6m) = €38m. The remaining financing balance is €59m − €38m = €21m.
  2. The separate step-up rate is 30/10,000 = 0.003. Additional coupon cash is €60m × 0.003 = €180,000.
  3. Report the allocation register and the €21m unallocated balance, and issue a separate verified target and €180,000 coupon notice. The assumed clauses create no cross-credit between reports.

Interview practice

Explain it aloud

Can an issuer fully allocate green funding and still miss its emissions target, and what evidence belongs in each report?

Saved on this device as you type.
Compare with a spoken answer

Yes. Earmarking financing for eligible assets and achieving an issuer-wide performance target are separate commitments. The green report reconciles net funds, allocated expenditure and any unallocated balance. The linked bond report verifies its defined KPI and applies its payment rule. Here the utility allocates €70m to eligible assets and also owes €0.20m extra on each of two coupons after its intensity target is missed.

Interviewer’s follow-up

Verified intensity falls by 5% while output increases 20% on a comparable basis. What happens to absolute emissions?

Compare with the follow-up answer

Absolute emissions are intensity times output. The new relative amount is 0.95 × 1.20 = 1.14, a 14% increase. Report that distinction and check the actual contract to see whether it uses intensity, absolute tonnes or both.
