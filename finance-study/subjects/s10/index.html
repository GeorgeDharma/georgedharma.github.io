S10Advanced finance

Project, Development and Sustainable Finance

A power plant can be socially useful and still fail to repay a dollar loan if it earns only local currency. Project finance looks at the asset’s contracts and cash flows as the main sources of repayment.

Subject worked example

A binding coverage test

A plant has €12m cash available in a year and the lender requires 1.5× coverage. Scheduled debt service is €9m.

Show the working

Maximum service allowed by the test is 12/1.5 = €8m. The scheduled amount gives only 1.33× coverage, so at least €1m of that year’s service must be reduced or otherwise addressed.

Course overview

What you will learn

Model a project waterfall, size debt against coverage, allocate risks and distinguish a financial return from a measurable public benefit.

Theory and assumptions

Limited-recourse finance, risk allocation, externalities and additionality. A project company ring-fences cash flows, while contracts assign construction, demand, currency and operating risks.
