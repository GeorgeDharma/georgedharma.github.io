New and expanded lessons

S10.11

Development appraisal and the residual land bid

S10.02 deals with construction interest and S10.05 sizes lenders’ claims. The distinct investment question arises before a developer buys land. The land price is solved from staged sales, build costs, contingencies and a required developer return.

  • Derive a maximum land offer from staged property development cash flows and purchaser costs.
  • Distinguish the price of land from project NPV and quantify a delay in unit sales.

S10.12

Green proceeds, sustainability targets and financial consequences

S10.07 covers concessionality and S10.09 measures avoided emissions. Those units do not reconcile a green financing allocation ledger or translate a separately verified issuer target into a contractual interest payment.

  • Reconcile an eligible asset register to net green proceeds and disclose unallocated funds.
  • Test a sustainability-linked target and calculate the amount and timing of a contractual coupon change.

S10.13

Conventional loans, murabaha sales and ijara sukuk

S10.08 already traces a lease certificate and contrasts two recovery routes. This lesson adds the actual ownership sequence in a murabaha purchase, compares its instalments with a conventional loan on the same equipment, and follows a separate sukuk from subscription to recovery.

  • Reconstruct who owns the asset and who owes each payment under a loan, a disclosed cost-plus sale and a lease-based certificate.
  • Compare financing returns on dated cash flows and calculate default recovery under expressly stated rights.

Public benchmarks

Massachusetts Institute of Technology, United States

11.431J Real Estate Finance and Investment, lecture notes

The lecture sequence builds real-estate cash-flow projections, DCF and NPV before considering leverage, the opportunity cost of capital and investment decisions.

Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors

Valuation of development property, first edition

The guidance distinguishes the residual land value from profit on a known-price site and explains development income, costs, profit allowance, timed cash flows and sensitivity.

University of Toronto, Canada

ENV1707H Climate Finance, Winter 2026 course syllabus

Outcomes include matching grants, project finance and other funding to technology stage, green-bond structural features, and the financial triggers of sustainability-linked lending.

International Capital Market Association

Green Bond Principles, June 2025

The voluntary guidelines distinguish eligible use of proceeds, project evaluation, management of funds and reporting, including tracked unallocated balances.

International Capital Market Association

Sustainability-Linked Bond Principles, June 2024

The five components connect material indicators, target calibration, financial characteristics, reporting and independent verification.

Universiti Teknologi MARA, Malaysia

IMU503 Advanced Fiqh Muamalat

The published course distinguishes commercial exchange contracts including murabahah and ijarah from partnership, agency and security contracts.

World Bank Group

Reference Guide: Islamic Finance for Infrastructure PPP Projects

The guide explains cost-plus sales, leases, agency responsibilities, purchase undertakings and the consequences of default or asset loss in project structures.

World Bank Group PPP Resource Center

Overview of Assets Recycling Through Islamic Finance

The overview traces subscriptions, asset and use rights, leases and rental distributions, distinguishing asset-backed enforcement from a structure primarily reliant on an obligor.
