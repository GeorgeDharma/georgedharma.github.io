Case 1 of 4 · 10 marks

A project reaches acceptance late

A rail project uses €90m for construction and €5m for reserves, financed by €30m equity and €65m debt. A completion guarantee covers the first €3m of qualifying overruns up to certification. The guarantee expressly excludes interest and operating demand. A €5m qualifying overrun coincides with a four-month delay on €60m principal drawn at 6% annual simple interest. No later compensation is payable.

Case 2 of 4 · 10 marks

A two-year solar loan

A project has annual CFADS of €16.5m and €18.15m, all paid at year-end. Senior coverage target is 1.5 and loan rate 10%. No fees, capitalized interest or balloon apply. At the start of year two a reserve balance of €1.0m is available and must be restored to €1.8m after scheduled debt service before any distribution.

Case 3 of 4 · 10 marks

Public risk and additional connections

A municipality supplies €2m first-loss protection on a rural distribution portfolio, with €8m private senior exposure. Losses are €0m (60%), €1m (20%) or €5m (20%). Each year the scheme promises an independently verified reliability improvement. A separate bidder says it could have financed a smaller rollout without public support.

Case 4 of 4 · 10 marks

Lease funding and public value

A transit project's lease vehicle receives €5m rent, pays €0.5m specified costs and adds €0.5m to a reserve on €40m certificate face. Separately, for a whole-life national appraisal, resource costs have PV €60m, users' gross benefits before fares €88m, domestic fares €22m, and noise costs €9m. For project-level financial appraisal use only fares and resource costs as stated.

Case 1 · 10 marks

A project reaches acceptance late

Part A 3 marks

Reconcile base funding and compute the uncovered construction overrun.

Base sources and uses equal €95m. The guarantee supplies €3m against the €5m overrun, leaving €2m construction cost uncovered.

Part B 4 marks

Calculate extra delay interest and aggregate additional funding needed after guarantee.

Delay interest is €60m × 6% × 4/12 = €1.2m. Together with €2m uncovered construction it creates €3.2m additional funding need before any other changes.

Part C 3 marks

The sponsor cites the same guarantee as protection from weak first-year traffic. Assess the claim and give one independent control.

The guarantee excludes demand and expires at certification. Inspect minimum revenue or availability terms, traffic downside capacity or operating reserves as separate protections.

Case 2 · 10 marks

A two-year solar loan

Part A 4 marks

Size the loan and show debt service each year.

Service ceilings are €11m and €12.1m. Discounting at 10% gives €11m/1.1 + €12.1m/1.1² = €20m initial debt.

Part B 3 marks

Roll forward principal through both years.

Year one interest is €2m and €9m principal is repaid, leaving €11m. Year two charges €1.1m interest and repays the final €11m.

Part C 3 marks

Find year-two distribution if no other restrictions apply and explain one effect of collecting the agreed tariff late.

Year-two cash after €12.1m service is €6.05m. A €0.8m reserve top-up leaves €5.25m distributable. Late collections can interrupt service or the reserve test despite an otherwise adequate agreed tariff.

Case 3 · 10 marks

Public risk and additional connections

Part A 4 marks

Allocate each state's losses and compute expected public and private losses.

Public/ private allocations are 0/0, 1/0 and 2/3 in millions. Expected public loss is €0.6m and expected private loss €0.6m. Together they equal €1.2m expected total loss.

Part B 3 marks

State the observed mobilization ratio and why it does not answer the counterfactual.

Nominal private/public commitment ratio is 8/2 = 4. It cannot establish which private commitments would have existed without support or which extra connections were delivered.

Part C 3 marks

Design an outcome check and a fiscal stress beyond the average.

Compare outage duration for eligible customers with a defined baseline or credible alternative rollout and verify metered results. Stress the €2m maximum alongside other guarantees triggered by the same local shock.

Case 4 · 10 marks

Lease funding and public value

Part A 3 marks

Calculate current distribution and distribution-to-face ratio.

The vehicle distributes €4m, equal to 10% of face in the stated period. Reserves are not available for the current distribution.

Part B 4 marks

Calculate project financial value, users' net benefit and national economic value.

Financial value is €22m − €60m = minus €38m. Users net €66m. Aggregate value after €9m noise cost is €19m. Fares cancel inside the national boundary.

Part C 3 marks

Can these positive investor distributions and economic value establish repayment of certificate principal? Identify two missing elements.

No. Examine remaining rental cash, final purchase or sale arrangements, counterparty capacity and enforceable asset rights. Social benefits need not generate cash available to the certificate issuer.

Additional exam cases

After the core paper, test the additional lectures below. Each case opens with an answer space and its own worked solution. Use the worked solutions to assess these cases separately from the 40-mark paper.

  1. S10.11 · Development appraisal and the residual land bid

    A one-year development sells for €10m at completion and pays €0.5m selling expense and €4.5m construction costs that day. It incurs €0.5m of non-land expenses today. Purchaser costs are 10% of land price and the return target is 10%. Find the ceiling and evaluate a €3.8m ask.

  2. S10.12 · Green proceeds, sustainability targets and financial consequences

    A green project issue yields €59m net. €24m fully eligible expenditure and €20m other eligible spending are documented, but €6m of the second group is already allocated. Separately, a €60m linked bond misses a verified target and its terms increase one full annual coupon by 30 basis points. What amounts belong in each report?

  3. S10.13 · Conventional loans, murabaha sales and ijara sukuk

    A financier buys a refrigeration unit for RM240,000, then sells it for RM276,000 payable in three annual RM92,000 instalments. The customer pays the first two and defaults at the last due date. Security yields RM68,000 net and there is no further recovery. Separately, a lease trust earns RM450,000 annual rent, pays RM90,000 owner costs and has RM6m of certificates. Quantify the price, loss and lease distribution.
