Theory midterm · 10 marks

A new line and the right discount rate

After lessons 1–5, allow 20 minutes. Explain the reasoning behind your answer.

A proposed line costs €100 today and is expected to produce a single €110 operating cash flow in one year. Its risk warrants 8%. A manager also proposes subtracting €3 interest from that cash flow and discounting at the same financing-adjusted rate.

  1. Part A · 4 marks

    Decide whether the operating project creates value and show the method.

  2. Part B · 3 marks

    Explain the treatment of interest.

  3. Part C · 3 marks

    In the frictionless financing-irrelevance model, what would adding debt change?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the midterm marking guide

Part A · 4 marks

Discount the operating cash once. Its present value is 110/1.08 = 101.85 and NPV is 1.85. Credit the dated flows, discount factor, result and acceptance under these assumptions.

Part B · 3 marks

A weighted capital cost applied to cash available to all capital providers already reflects financing costs. Subtracting interest from that cash and using the same rate would count financing twice. Equity cash needs a corresponding equity rate.

Part C · 3 marks

It reallocates the operating cash flow and risk between debt and equity. Total firm value stays tied to the assets under the assumptions. Taxes, distress costs and changes in investment policy can change that conclusion.

Problem 1

Unlevered project value is €110m and immediate investment is €100m. Financing adds a €4m tax benefit and €2m of expected costs. Find adjusted NPV.

Saved on this device as you type.
Show the worked solution

Unlevered NPV is €10m. Adding financing effects gives €10m + €4m − €2m = €12m.

Problem 2

If cheap debt replaces expensive equity, must the company’s overall cost of capital fall?

Saved on this device as you type.
Show the worked solution

No. Remaining equity becomes riskier and may require a higher return. Taxes, distress and borrowing constraints determine the outcome beyond the frictionless benchmark.

Assessment criteria
  • Apply the right theory and state the assumptions.
  • Show each step with consistent units, dates and currency.
  • Interpret the result, test an alternative and explain what could change the decision.
Suggested academic marking scheme

Use these weights when assessing the complete subject. The four problem sets collect the written problems from individual lectures. You can use the 10-mark midterm and the 40-mark subject paper with the weights below.

Problem sets 25%
Four sets per standard subject. Show reasoning, calculations, units and checks. The two practice problems in each subject are worked samples for these sets.
Theory midterm 20%
After the first half of the subject. Explain or derive a model, identify assumptions and state what would change if one failed.
Seminar paper or laboratory 25%
During the second half. Apply the theory to a case, dataset or implementation. Address an alternative explanation and document how to reproduce the result.
Individual final examination 30%
At the end of the subject. Solve an unfamiliar problem, interpret the result and critique a claim. M&A and lending each include a 30-mark examination question with a marking guide.
