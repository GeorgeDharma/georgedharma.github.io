Corporate Finance and Capital Structure

A good project and a good financing plan answer different questions. Corporate finance studies investment, borrowing and payouts, including when these decisions affect one another.

Subject worked example

Financing a project

A business uses 60% equity costing 10% and 40% debt costing 5%. Assume a 25% tax rate and immediately usable interest deductions.

Show the working

The weighted average cost of capital is 0.60 × 10% + 0.40 × 5% × 0.75 = 7.5%. Using it for a new project also requires comparable risk and financing policy.

Course overview

What you will learn

Estimate project value, explain why financing may change value and distinguish a company’s average funding cost from a project-specific hurdle rate.

Theory and assumptions

Modigliani–Miller, tax shields, financial distress, agency costs and the pecking order. Start with a frictionless benchmark and add one friction at a time.
