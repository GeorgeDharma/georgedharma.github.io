Pricing a forfaiting offer for export instalments

Discount individually dated export notes and compare net proceeds with a cash sale.

  3. Define the boundary of nonrecourse

  5. Equal face values fetch different cash prices

Theory and terminology

Non-recourse export forfaiting

discounting export bills · credit risk transfer

What you should be able to explain

  • Discount individually dated export notes and compare net proceeds with a cash sale.
  • Identify which risks transfer in a nonrecourse purchase and what obligations the exporter retains.

Locate the payment claim

A foreign purchaser of capital equipment may want instalments aligned with future production. A promissory note is the buyer’s written obligation to pay a defined amount at a defined date. A forfaiter purchases eligible obligations for immediate cash. A bank guarantee or aval, a bank undertaking on a note or bill under the applicable legal framework, can improve the credit on which the financier relies.

Factoring frequently serves an ongoing book of shorter-term trade receivables and may include collection services. Forfaiting can be arranged for one larger export contract with a schedule of payments. In both cases read the actual recourse terms. A borrowing-base loan leaves the company owing the lender even if its receivables provide collateral.

Turn a schedule into a cash price

Write one row for each payment date, face amount, time remaining, discount convention and present value. The examples quote an effective annual yield, so divide by a compound factor. Some market quotations use a discount on face, specific day counts, grace days or commitment fees. A quoted percentage alone cannot identify the proceeds.

Deduct fees at the agreed time. Compare with the net cash from an immediate-payment sale on the same date, holding manufacturing costs and other obligations constant. A deferred invoice carrying a high nominal price can still produce less cash than a smaller immediate order after discounting and fees.

Define the boundary of nonrecourse

In a valid nonrecourse sale, an eligible failure to pay by the importer or designated guarantor is ordinarily the financier’s risk under the contract. The exporter may still be liable for false representations, invalid or defective documents, or its separate obligations to deliver machinery or honor warranties. Check the exact exceptions, governing law and conditions for purchase.

An early indicative quote is different from a committed purchase on a fixed rate. Obtain agreement on the buyer, guarantor bank, country, dates, currency and documentary wording before promising a long credit period. A note in another currency can also leave the exporter exposed between agreeing its cash price and fixing conversion.

Method

Build the result

Net proceeds = Σ[Fₜ/(1+y)ᵗ] − f.

Fₜ is each note’s face amount in currency due t years after today, y the agreed effective annual yield and f the cash fee retained at purchase. Use the exact day-count and compounding convention of the quote for a real transaction.

  1. Project contractual payment dates and amounts, including any stated interest in the face amounts.
  2. Discount each amount for its own term, then add the present values.
  3. Subtract today’s fee once and compare with the equally dated cash alternative. Check the documentary conditions separately.
Worked case

A machine-tool order pays in three notes

An exporter receives three guaranteed $400,000 notes due in exactly one, two and three years. A forfaiter quotes a 6% effective annual yield, no grace days and a $9,000 fee paid today. The buyer alternatively offers $1,055,000 cash now for the same machine and warranty.

Working

  1. The first note is worth 400,000/1.06 = $377,358.49.
  2. The second is 400,000/1.06² = $355,998.58 and the third 400,000/1.06³ = $335,847.71.
  3. Their total discounted value is $1,069,204.78. After the $9,000 fee, immediate proceeds are $1,060,204.78.
  4. The forfaited route produces $5,204.78 more than the cash alternative. The $1.2m future face amount overstates the comparable cash value by almost $140,000 after fees.

Decision and interpretation

If the guarantee and note wording are acceptable and both routes impose the same other costs, the instalment order produces slightly more cash. A buyer amendment or additional legal fee above the margin could reverse the decision.

Quick check
An exporter sells a valid guaranteed note without recourse for payment default. Which obligation ordinarily still depends on its own sale agreement?

A sale of the payment claim transfers the agreed credit exposure. The warranty on goods remains an obligation under the sale contract.

Written practice

Independent exam problem

A different exporter can sell two €150,000 notes due in one and two years at an 8% effective annual yield and a €2,500 fee, or accept €266,000 cash today. Which gives more immediately?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the worked solution
  1. The first note is worth €138,888.89. The second is 150,000/1.08² = €128,600.82.
  2. Net proceeds are 138,888.89 + 128,600.82 − 2,500 = €264,989.71.
  3. The cash offer exceeds the forfaited proceeds by €1,010.29, assuming the same remaining commercial obligations.

Interview practice

Explain it aloud

How can a machinery exporter offer a buyer several years to pay but collect cash today?

Saved on this device as you type.
Compare with a spoken answer

It can arrange a forfaiter to purchase eligible future payment obligations, often buyer notes backed by an acceptable bank. The financier pays their discounted value less agreed fees. The exporter gives up future collections and usually transfers specified payment credit risk once the documents and contractual conditions are satisfied.

Interviewer’s follow-up

If the bank’s aval required by the commitment is still unsigned, can the exporter count the quoted proceeds as unconditional cash funding?

Compare with the follow-up answer

No. A required purchase condition remains outstanding. The treasurer should show contingent funding, obtain the executed undertaking or renegotiate acceptance before relying on the cash for a fixed payment.
