Subject worked example

Cash in the right currency

An importer owes USD 110,000 in 30 days. It has €105,000, including €10,000 needed for payroll. A forward quote is USD 1.10 per euro.

Show the working

The forward fixes a €100,000 invoice cost. Only €95,000 is available after reserving payroll, so the firm still needs €5,000 funding. Fixing the exchange rate solves only one part of the problem.

Course overview

What you will learn

Construct a multicurrency funding plan, compare trade payment methods and quantify residual risk under a hedge policy.

Theory and assumptions

Cash-conversion cycles, liquidity buffers, hedging under financial frictions and trade credit as a financing contract. A hedge has value when it protects funding or investment capacity.
