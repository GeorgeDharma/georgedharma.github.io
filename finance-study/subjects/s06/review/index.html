New and expanded lessons

S06.11

Locating transit risk and comparing delivered cost under Incoterms

The payment and documentary-credit lessons establish when cash is due and what documents support payment. A separate shipping-term analysis is needed to locate transit risk, insurance duties and the actual buyer cash budget.

  • Distinguish where transit risk transfers from the location to which freight is paid.
  • Compare two delivered-cost quotations and compute the ordinary CIP insurance minimum.

S06.12

Pricing a forfaiting offer for export instalments

The receivables lesson calculates availability on an operating line and compares short-term finance. This chapter prices a nonrecourse purchase of several export instalments and identifies which documentary and performance obligations survive.

  • Discount individually dated export notes and compare net proceeds with a cash sale.
  • Identify which risks transfer in a nonrecourse purchase and what obligations the exporter retains.

S06.13

Measuring the hours of risk in a foreign-exchange settlement

The treasury hedge and liquidity lessons handle market movements and margin needs. The market infrastructure lesson covers securities delivery versus payment. This chapter identifies the two currency legs and measures when overlapping trades consume a treasury counterparty limit.

  • Calculate peak FX principal at risk from cancellation deadlines and confirmed final receipts.
  • Compare payment-versus-payment settlement, netting and a liquidity facility.

Public benchmarks

İzmir University of Economics

ITF 412 Financing International Trade and Project

The learning outcomes combine documentary credits and collections with commercial, political and currency risk. They ask students to compare factoring, forfaiting and other financing methods in Türkiye and internationally.

International Chamber of Commerce Academy

CPT & CIP Incoterms® 2020 explained: Key differences

The ICC teaching material separates the carrier handover point from the named paid-to destination, identifies who arranges carriage and cargo insurance and explains the ordinary CIP coverage requirements.

International Trade Administration, US Department of Commerce

Trade Finance Guide

The guide compares collection, documentary credit, factoring and forfaiting. Its forfaiting chapter follows export notes or bills, bank guarantees and the cash trade-off when an exporter sells a future payment stream.

Basel Committee on Banking Supervision

Basel Consolidated Guidelines, RMA20 Foreign exchange risks

The supervisory guidance distinguishes principal, replacement-cost and liquidity risks, defines the period starting when payment can no longer be canceled unilaterally, and addresses the use of payment-versus-payment settlement.

Foreign Exchange Committee, hosted by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York

Understanding FX Settlement Risk

The presentation maps the period between unilateral cancellation and final receipt, demonstrates how full principal can be exposed, and compares settlement arrangements with and without payment versus payment.
