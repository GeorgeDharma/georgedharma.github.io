Theory midterm · 10 marks

A group cash total that cannot make a payment

After lessons 1–5, allow 20 minutes. Explain the reasoning behind your answer.

The parent owes €12m in three days. It has €4m of freely available cash, a subsidiary has €10m of restricted balances, and a committed €6m line is drawable tomorrow. The subsidiary can remit €1m before the cutoff.

  1. Part A · 4 marks

    Prepare the available-cash bridge and calculate the gap.

  2. Part B · 3 marks

    Explain why the group’s bank-statement total can mislead.

  3. Part C · 3 marks

    How would you test a proposed rescue through intercompany netting?

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the midterm marking guide

Part A · 4 marks

Count 4 of parent cash, 6 available from the line and 1 remittable. Usable resources are 11 and the shortfall is 1. Credit the dates, treatment of restricted money, total and gap.

Part B · 3 marks

Legal entities, currencies, local controls, collateral and cutoffs determine who can use balances. A positive group cash sum does not transfer money to the entity that must settle.

Part C · 3 marks

List reciprocal obligations by counterparty, currency and value date. Verify agreements and transfer permissions. Then recalculate actual external settlements. If the parent’s €12m is an external payment with no offset, netting internal balances alone cannot remove it.

Problem 1

A firm has 50 inventory days, 40 collection days and 30 supplier-payment days. Estimate its cash-conversion cycle.

Saved on this device as you type.
Show the worked solution

50 + 40 − 30 = 60 days. This is an average measure and can understate peak seasonal funding.

Assessment criteria
  • Apply the right theory and state the assumptions.
  • Show each step with consistent units, dates and currency.
  • Interpret the result, test an alternative and explain what could change the decision.
Suggested academic marking scheme

Use these weights when assessing the complete subject. The four problem sets collect the written problems from individual lectures. You can use the 10-mark midterm and the 40-mark subject paper with the weights below.

Problem sets 25%
Four sets per standard subject. Show reasoning, calculations, units and checks. The two practice problems in each subject are worked samples for these sets.
Theory midterm 20%
After the first half of the subject. Explain or derive a model, identify assumptions and state what would change if one failed.
Seminar paper or laboratory 25%
During the second half. Apply the theory to a case, dataset or implementation. Address an alternative explanation and document how to reproduce the result.
Individual final examination 30%
At the end of the subject. Solve an unfamiliar problem, interpret the result and critique a claim. M&A and lending each include a 30-mark examination question with a marking guide.
