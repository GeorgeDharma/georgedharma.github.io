Professional workshop · S06

Fund payroll and hedge an exporter’s documentary-credit receipt

Aster Instrumentation ships on day 0. Its French treasury entity must fund payroll now, present valid documents and convert a large US-dollar receipt on day 30. Prepare a dated cash position, borrowing-base certificate, FX instruction and exception plan for a late presentation. Use the fictional credit and facility rules supplied here. All euro or dollar amounts are in thousands unless a different unit is shown.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Group treasury analyst preparing the dealing and liquidity instructions
What to submit
A treasury cash-position report, approved-deal ticket and independent settlement checklist, followed by a liquidity escalation note to the group treasurer.

From inputs to decision

01

Reconcile usable money for the correct paying entity

The group sees a large positive statement balance but must honor instructions already released and preserve pledged cash. The uncleared deposit cannot be used to approve another payment.

  1. Begin with €1,200k and deduct €180k outstanding instructed transfers. Deduct €240k pledged cash, leaving €780k genuinely free for new uses on day 0. Keep the €90k deposit out until clearing is confirmed.
  2. Payroll and freight total €730k. After those payments cash would be €50k. Maintaining the €200k operating floor needs an additional €150k by the payment cutoff.

Day 0 minimum incremental funding instruction is €150k, verified against the date of actual availability.

02

Certify the receivables borrowing base before drawing

An unused headline commitment can be larger than collateral-backed availability. An independent certificate avoids telling the CFO that unusable limits finance payroll.

  1. Eligible domestic receivables are €2,000k − €350k − €250k − €150k = €1,250k. At 80% advance less the €100k reserve, borrowing-base limit is €900k.
  2. Take the lower of €1,200k commitment and €900k base. Subtract €400k existing debt to obtain €500k additional current availability. Draw €150k by cutoff, leaving €350k unused headroom.

Total drawn reaches €550k and day-0 closing free cash is exactly €200k.

03

Roll the intermediate date and restore capacity

A temporary draw can be returned as domestic cash clears. The day-30 stress must start from actual balances after that repayment, rather than an optimistic gross bank balance.

  1. Day 10 starts at €200k and receives €350k before paying €160k, producing €390k. Repay €150k of incremental debt while retaining €240k.
  2. Facility usage returns to the original €400k. Base-case incremental headroom returns to €500k while eligibility remains unchanged. Do not include the unconfirmed €90k deposit in the €240k.

Day 30 opens with €240k free, €400k original drawings and €500k base-case additional capacity.

04

Control the credit presentation

The payment date exists only if stipulated documents comply or an effective waiver is obtained. The sales invoice is insufficient evidence for a euro liquidity commitment.

  1. Shipment on day 0 gives a presentation deadline of day 7 under the allowance. Day 15 credit expiry is later and does not extend that deadline. Assign the shipping team the transport document, finance the invoice and the independent reviewer the comparison against credit terms.
  2. In the base case, complete and accurately present the day-5 set before cutoff and obtain bank acknowledgment. In stress, day-9 documents are late. Escalate for a valid waiver and move forecast receipt to day 45 on the case assumption.

Record day-30 dollars as confirmed only against the presentation and bank status. Maintain a separate day-45 scenario pending effective waiver.

05

Prepare the FX ticket and budget bridge

The exporter needs dollars for a supplier payment on the same day. Hedging gross revenue would create a larger position than the euro cash it is trying to protect.

  1. Net dollars on day 30 are US$1,200k − US$300k = US$900k. Seventy percent is US$630k, below the 80% policy limit of US$720k. Ticket direction is sell US$630k and receive €573.3k on day 30.
  2. Remaining US$270k converts at €0.86 per dollar, giving €232.2k. Net euro receipt is €573.3k + €232.2k − €2.5k = €803k. Budget on net volume is €810k. Covered rate contributes +€6.3k, open spot −€10.8k and fees −€2.5k, total −€7k.

Base day-30 cash closes at €240k + €803k − €580k = €463k, €263k above the required floor.

06

Stress the hedge when shipment cash is delayed

A binding forward can settle before the customer pays. Buying dollars to settle that forward and the supplier translates a delayed receipt into an immediate euro funding need.

  1. With no day-30 receipt, the company must source US$630k forward delivery plus US$300k supplier payment, totaling US$930k. Buying those dollars at €0.86 costs €799.8k. The forward pays €573.3k euros, and the fee is €2.5k.
  2. Net dollar-related euro cash is €573.3k − €799.8k − €2.5k = −€229k. After operating payments, cash before incremental funding is €240k − €229k − €580k = −€569k. To reach the €200k floor requires €769k incremental funding.

Even with the full €500k base-case available line, unfilled day-30 need would be €269k.

07

Combine the late-credit and collateral shocks

The most important protection is funding drawable when the disruption occurs. Modeling the delay while keeping ordinary eligibility would understate the actual shortfall.

  1. An additional €250k loses eligibility, so the pool falls to €1,000k. Revised borrowing base is 80% × €1,000k − €100k = €700k. With €400k used, only €300k additional credit remains.
  2. Against €769k incremental need, the combined stress leaves €469k uncovered. Secure an independent amount of at least €469k by day 30 or a binding equivalent change to payment timing. Test actual currency, draw conditions and transfer cutoffs.

Escalate the €469k timing exposure immediately if the day-7 deadline is threatened. Do not assume projected day-45 dollars pay day-30 commitments.

08

Release, reconcile and monitor the transaction

One analyst should not be able to create a trade, approve settlement and independently certify compliance. The desk also needs an actionable response to forecast changes.

  1. An authorized dealer matches the US$630k instruction to the day-30 underlying. Independent operations confirms trade direction, rate, date, counterparty and settlement instructions using established contacts. Separate bank approval releases payments.
  2. Reconcile actual sale, supplier outflow, forward settlement, fee and bank balances to the €803k combined receipt in the base case. On delay, assess a permitted forward roll or separate dollar line with costs and approvals rather than automatically renewing the trade.

The CFO receives a base settlement instruction, a funded day-0 payroll and a quantified escalation trigger before the documentary deadline.

Completed document

Treasury dealing and liquidity mandate | Aster Instrumentation

Payments and funding

Draw €150k today against €500k verified additional availability. Settle payroll and freight while retaining €200k. On day 10, after €350k clears and €160k pays, repay the incremental €150k. Projected cash for day 30 is then €240k.

Dealing ticket

Sell US$630k against euro for day 30 at €0.91 per dollar. Reference is the US$900k net receipt after the supplier payment. Coverage is 70%, within the 80% mandate. Independent operations must confirm the instruction and approved bank details.

Documentary dependency

The operational presentation deadline is day 7. Submit the complete accurate documents on day 5 and obtain bank acknowledgment. An incomplete or late set requires immediate escalation and an independently confirmed revised payment date.

Liquidity exception and authorization

The compliant base closes with €463k. Late payment plus the collateral shock produces €469k unfinanced after existing facilities. Secure the backup before relying on it, or obtain documented creditor timing changes. A forward extension requires separate dealing authority, counterparty terms and cash-flow comparison.
