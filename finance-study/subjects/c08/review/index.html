New and expanded lessons

C08.11

Bank resolution, creditor bail-in and continuity of payments

Banking already explains runs, capital shortfalls and emergency liquidity, while restructuring maps ordinary corporate priority. The earlier material does not reconstruct a failed bank’s balance sheet after writedown and conversion, benchmark recoveries against liquidation and check next-day payment continuity.

  • Separate loss recognition from the conversion of creditors into new equity.
  • Test the liquidation safeguard and a post-resolution cash gap using distinct valuations.

C08.12

How a non-life insurer earns premiums, reserves for claims and uses reinsurance

The banking unit uses insurance to explain pooling and deposit guarantees, and corporate cases mention insurance policies. It does not construct a casualty insurer’s earned premium, incurred losses, combined ratio and net result after proportional reinsurance.

  • Reconcile written premiums and paid claims with their earned and incurred amounts.
  • Calculate how quota-share reinsurance and commissions alter underwriting profit and retained loss.

C08.13

Customer due diligence, beneficial ownership and transaction monitoring

The credit course verifies borrowers and the markets course investigates trading conduct. The earlier banking lessons do not trace beneficial owners through multiple shareholder paths, establish an expected account profile or document a new activity alert and escalation decision.

  • Trace interests to natural persons, aggregate independent paths and test control by other means.
  • Use an evidence-based customer profile to resolve or escalate unexpected account activity.

Public benchmarks

University of Warwick, United Kingdom

LA905-20 International Banking & Securities Regulation

This graduate module moves from banking supervision and capital adequacy to financial crisis management, bank resolution, insolvency and deposit insurance.

Financial Stability Board

Key Attributes of Effective Resolution Regimes for Financial Institutions, revised 2024

The standard sets out continuity of vital functions, powers such as transfer and bail-in, funding for resolution and the comparison with liquidation for creditor safeguards.

University of Northern Iowa, United States

Insurance Operations

The teaching and assessment material distinguishes life and casualty operations and examines losses, expenses and a financial-basis combined ratio using earned premiums.

Society of Actuaries

Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, 2019 Supplement

The supplement explains proportional risk transfer, shared premiums and claims, and the ceding commission paid to a primary insurer.

Financial Action Task Force

The FATF Recommendations

Recommendation 10 addresses verified customer identity, beneficial ownership, business purpose and ongoing diligence. Recommendations 20 and 21 cover suspicious-transaction reporting and confidentiality.

Hong Kong Institute of Bankers

ECF on Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism, Programme Handbook

The professional syllabus covers risk-based customer due diligence and transaction monitoring in banking, including assessing customer information and responding to suspicious activity.
