Professional workshop · C08

Approve a rate hedge and a thirty-day funding plan for a commercial bank

On a hypothetical reporting date, Harbor Commercial Bank’s asset–liability committee asks whether it can absorb a two-percentage-point rate rise, a thirty-day funding shock and an €18m credit write-off. Submit a one-year earnings-at-risk decision, an executable cash plan and a post-stress capital bridge. All amounts are EUR millions unless otherwise stated. Use the board’s teaching scenario rather than labeling it a regulatory calculation. For the earnings test use the unchanged beginning business mix. For the separate joint liquidity and capital test post the specified transactions on the balance sheet. The data do not establish full-year earnings on the post-runoff balance sheet.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Bank asset–liability management analyst
What to submit
Prepare an ALCO decision paper for the treasurer, chief risk officer and board risk committee. The dealing desk needs the hedge direction and size. Treasury needs the pledged and sold securities reconciled without double counting. Capital management needs the minimum remediation and a proposed operating buffer.

From inputs to decision

01

Reconcile the opening balance sheet and earnings drivers

A wrong-side entry for deposits or loan assets distorts both the rate and the liquidity calculations.

  1. Opening assets are 50 reserves + 100 securities + 300 fixed loans + 150 floating loans = 600. Deposits 450 plus wholesale debt 90 and equity 60 also total 600. The starting CET1 ratio under the supplied classroom weights is 60/400 = 15%.
  2. Gross annual interest income is 100 × 3% + 300 × 4% + 150 × 5% = 22.50. Interest expense is 300 × 1% + 150 × 2% + 90 × 3% = 8.70. NII is 13.80. On 550 of interest-earning assets, the initial margin is about 2.51%.

Carry interest-earning assets, immediate cash and qualifying capital as three separate quantities. Tomorrow’s payment needs cash on its settlement date.

02

Apply rate pass-through by product and fixing date

The loans with the fastest interest response still lag the deposit and wholesale costs, so an interest-rate rise can erode first-year earnings.

  1. Floating loans earn an extra 150 × 2% × 0.5 = 1.50 in year one. Retail funding costs rise 300 × 2% × 35% = 2.10, corporate deposits cost 150 × 2% × 75% = 2.25 more, and wholesale cost rises 90 × 2% = 1.80.
  2. Total added funding cost is 6.15. Unhedged stressed first-year NII is 13.80 + 1.50 − 6.15 = 9.15, or about 1.66% of unchanged earning assets. This falls 0.85 below the board test.

Show the timing difference. With a full twelve months of resets in a subsequent otherwise identical year, the loan benefit would be 3.00, but this cannot be recognized in the current one-year limit test.

03

Size and specify the earnings hedge

A hedge that receives floating interest compensates the bank when its short-term funding costs rise. Paying floating would worsen the very exposure under review.

  1. The illustrative swap generates 2% of notional in the rising-rate scenario. Minimum notional is 0.85/0.02 = 42.50. Whole €10m lots require a €50m receive-floating/pay-fixed swap.
  2. This contributes 1.00, bringing the tested first-year NII to 10.15. If the reference stays at its initial level, there is no incremental net coupon under these assumptions. A comparable rate fall would create a net swap payment.

Authorize €50m as a scenario earnings hedge and require a separate market-value and collateral sensitivity before assuming the desk has hedged all interest-rate risks.

04

Build the stress withdrawal and drawdown ladder

The relevant cash need includes customers exercising committed lines as deposits leave. Only unrestricted reserves above the operating minimum can be spent.

  1. Retail outflows are 300 × 8% = 24. Corporate outflows are 150 × 30% = 45. Unrolled wholesale funding is 90 × 40% = 36. Add 20 of new loan drawings for gross outflows of 125. Contractual cash inflows of 15 reduce the need to 110.
  2. Opening reserves of 50 include 10 kept for settlement. Only 40 is available to reduce the new funding requirement, leaving a €70m gap before contingent actions.

This is an internal 30-day stress measure with bespoke runoff assumptions. Actual LCR, currency and intraday limits require their own contractual and regulatory definitions.

05

Assign collateral to exactly one funding action

A security pledged to secure the funding line cannot also be sold for proceeds. The treasury plan must name how much remains unencumbered after execution.

  1. Pledge 75 book of prepositioned securities at an 80% advance to receive 60 of secured cash and a new 60 liability. Remaining gap is 10. From the other 25 book available, sell 10/0.94 = 10.6383 book to raise 10 cash.
  2. The sale realizes a 0.6383 loss. Securities remaining are 100 − 10.6383 = 89.3617 book, including 75 pledged and 14.3617 unpledged. Opening cash 50 + funding 60 + sale 10 + inflows 15 − gross outflows 125 = closing 10.

An alternative relying solely on sales for 70 would require 74.4681 book and recognize 4.4681 loss, assuming sales of those otherwise unpledged pieces were available. Secured funding conserves capital, but creates future refinancing and encumbrance risk.

06

Recalculate the common equity constraint

Paying deposits from reserves can reduce both an asset and liability without reducing capital, while a discounted sale and a loss on loans do consume economic and regulatory equity in this case.

  1. Post the separately specified credit loss of 18 and realized security loss 0.6383 to starting CET1 of 60. Post-stress capital before injection is 41.3617. The loan loss itself is an accounting charge here, not an additional same-day withdrawal.
  2. RWA rise by 20 from newly drawn loans and fall by 18 on the write-off, ending 402. Ratio is 41.3617/402 = 10.289%. The board’s 10.5% floor needs 42.21 capital, leaving a shortfall of about 0.8483.

Authorize the €2m follow-on capital contribution with proof of timely legal completion. This brings CET1 to 43.3617 and the ratio to 10.786%, leaving roughly €1.152m of capital above the board floor.

07

Document the linked controls and remaining stress tests

The bank can arrange immediate cash and still fail a capital floor. It can meet a positive income floor in one rate scenario while remaining exposed to faster deposit repricing.

  1. Treasury must verify which 75 of securities are prepositioned, confirm immediate facility access and obtain firm sale bids on the unpledged pool. Observe daily cumulative cash against the 10 minimum. If 15 of inflows is delayed, rerun the ladder before counting it.
  2. Risk management must report corporate and retail deposit beta, wholesale renewal, collateral haircuts, security sale loss, actual use of committed customer credit and the owner capital settlement. Quantify the reverse-rate effect and economic value of equity outside the reported first-year NII test.

Escalate to the CFO and chair if projected cash falls below 10, the eligible 2 of capital is delayed or withdrawn, or the one-year shocked NII falls below 10. Prepare the next maturity schedule for the new secured borrowing.

Completed document

Asset–liability committee resolution and treasury mandate

Rate decision

Authorize a €50m one-year receive-floating/pay-fixed swap at the specified par terms and require normal pre-trade counterparty review. The approved rate-rise test improves from €9.15m unhedged to €10.15m NII. Deposit repricing and the delayed reset on €150m floating loans drive the exposure. This resolution covers the earnings target in the specified upward shock.

Liquidity plan

Preposition €75m book securities for €60m secured borrowing. Sell approximately €10.638m other book securities to raise €10m cash and maintain €10m reserves after the thirty-day stress. Treasury must verify collateral access, sale timing and separate security identifiers. Arrange a takeout plan for the new secured borrowing.

Capital condition

Record €18m credit loss and €0.638m security sale loss. With €402m stressed RWA, projected CET1 is 10.289%, below the board’s 10.5% threshold. Obtain the verified €2m common equity injection, giving 10.786%, before representing the bank as back within the capital limit. A temporary escalation is required while the transfer remains pending.

Control and ownership

Treasury owns daily settlement and encumbrance checks. The head of market risk owns deposit beta, swap sensitivity and an independent economic-value test. Credit owns the loss estimate and commitment draws. Finance owns qualifying capital confirmation. A worsening input must trigger a new linked calculation rather than use of the existing headline ratios.

The extra twenty percentage points of pass-through cost 300 × 0.02 × 0.20 = 1.20. Deduct this from hedged NII 10.15 to obtain €8.95m, below the floor by €1.05m.
