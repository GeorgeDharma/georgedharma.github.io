Case 1 · 10 marks

A cash model and its spreadsheet controls

Part A 4 marks

Calculate closing cash and headroom for the €10,000 revenue scenario at 70% and 80%, then solve the minimum collection rate.

At 70% the close is €4,000 + €7,000 − €10,000 = €1,000 and headroom −€500. At 80%, close €2,000 and headroom +€500. Required rate solves €4,000 + €10,000c − €10,000 = €1,500, giving 75%. Award for both cash outcomes, headroom and threshold.

Part B 3 marks

Write the receipts formula in B4 and the copied formula in C5. Calculate the latter result.

Use =$A4*B$3 as the first formula. Copying produces =$A5*C$3. The result is €8,000 × 0.80 = €6,400. Award for correct row and column anchoring and result.

Part C 3 marks

Design three independent checks for the default scenario and an error case.

At exactly 75% the €10,000 scenario must close at €1,500. Raising collection one percentage point raises cash €100. A missing collection share should follow a defined error or missing-data policy rather than automatically becoming zero. Equivalent independently reasoned instrument, corner or negative tests earn credit.

Case 2 · 10 marks

Joining loans and replayed payments

Part A 4 marks

Calculate total rows and principal after a naive inner and a naive left join.

The original has €150. Inner join expands the two X loans into four rows worth €200 and loses Y. Left join includes the Y loan once, yielding five rows and €250. Award for both counts and totals.

Part B 3 marks

Describe and validate a corrected portfolio report with complete principal coverage.

Resolve X to one verified applicable label and left-join all facilities. Keep Y explicitly unlabelled. Output must retain L1, L2 and L3 once each, totaling €150. Confirm unmatched ID Y and individual facility counts in addition to the aggregate.

Part C 3 marks

Find the correct and naive ledger balances. What should happen if P1 arrives again with a different amount?

Opening €100 + €30 − €12 = €118. Blindly adding the replay gives €148. A repeated ID with different content is a conflict or correction and requires a stated resolution procedure before changing the balance.

Case 3 · 10 marks

Corporate actions and stale marks

Part A 4 marks

Calculate local total return, final euro wealth and the euro return on the split share position.

Local ending wealth is 2 × ($52 + $1) = $106, so total return is 6%. Final euro wealth is €90.10, compared with €90 initially. Euro return is €0.10/€90 ≈ 0.1111%. Award for split-adjusted wealth, local return, euro conversion and euro return.

Part B 3 marks

For the separate portfolio, calculate full return if the suspended holding is marked unchanged and bound it when that return could be −10% to +5%.

Zero-fill gives 0.60 × 4% = 2.4%. Bounds are 2.4% − 4% = −1.6% and 2.4% + 2% = 4.4%. The bounds retain the suspended position’s original weight.

Part C 3 marks

Specify separate return-function outcomes for known equal positive prices, a missing end price and a known end price of zero with a positive start.

Equal verified prices give a numeric zero price return. A missing end price yields unknown under the stated policy. A verified zero ending price gives −100%. These cases have different meanings for aggregation and must remain distinguishable.

Case 4 · 10 marks

Reconstructing a point-in-time strategy

Part A 4 marks

What can a close-only strategy know about the March statistic at the 8 April close, at the 9 April close and at a close after the revision?

Both stated closes occur at 14:00 UTC. On 8 April the release arrives ten minutes late, so no March figure is eligible if no earlier release exists. On 9 April it may use 80. After 12 April 10:00 UTC it may use 86 at an eligible close. Award for conversion and all three selections.

Part B 3 marks

Calculate how the backfill changes the equal-weight mean and say what evidence distinguishes it from a formula change.

The original mean is 12/3 = 4%. Including −4% gives 8/4 = 2%, a −2-percentage-point change. Compare stable IDs and per-asset records across input snapshots while holding method and code revision fixed.

Part C 3 marks

Describe a record design allowing another analyst to reproduce a historical as-of decision and the published mean.

Retain reference period, release and ingestion timestamps with zones, version identifiers, original input snapshot or checksum, universe inclusion and code/parameter versions. Select the most recent eligible vintage before each decision and recreate the numeric table and chart from the very same derived records. Award for time/version, input identity and executable processing.
