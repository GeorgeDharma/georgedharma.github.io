New and expanded lessons

F05.11

Compare each price with its own previous observation

F05 teaches grouping, joins, as-of decisions and returns. A row-preserving SQL window gives learners an executable way to derive each instrument's prior observation, check elapsed time and contrast a trailing-row frame with a fixed calendar interval.

  • Construct a prior-price return without mixing instruments or losing the first reportable day's history.
  • Explain the difference between the previous recorded row and the previous scheduled trading session.

F05.12

Prevent half a transfer from reaching the ledger

F05 tests replayed records and reconciles totals after calculation. This addition gives an executable transaction that posts two dependent cash movements together, distinguishes a genuine retry from a conflicting ID and remains coherent when a failure interrupts the work.

  • Commit or roll back a balanced two-account movement as one operation.
  • Handle duplicate requests safely and detect a reused ID that describes a different transfer.

F05.13

Turn a supplier's CSV into trustworthy money and identifiers

F05 handles missing values, duplicate keys and audit controls in separate tasks. This chapter gives the raw-to-typed boundary its own executable validator, including a declared input dialect, locale-specific amount syntax, preserved identifiers, quarantine and a count-and-money reconciliation.

  • Parse and validate a payment feed without losing leading zeros, local amount signs or date meaning.
  • Separate accepted, replayed and rejected input records and reconcile their distinct controls.
