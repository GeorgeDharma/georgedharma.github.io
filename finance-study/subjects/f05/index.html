Financial Computing, Data and Reproducibility

A convincing calculation can be wrong because one invoice appeared twice or a price was recorded after the trade. Financial computing makes these errors visible and the analysis repeatable.

The duplicate join

Course overview

What you will learn

Build the same result in a spreadsheet and code, join dated records safely, and hand another learner a reproducible calculation.

Theory and assumptions

Relational keys, transformations and numerical checks. A data row must have a declared meaning, such as one security on one date, before aggregation.
