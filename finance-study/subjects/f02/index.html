Subject worked example

A fair gamble

With utility equal to the square root of final wealth, compare a certain €100 with equal chances of €64 and €144.

Show the working

The gamble’s expected wealth is €104. Its expected utility is (8 + 12)/2 = 10, equal to the utility of €100. Its certainty equivalent is €100 and its risk premium is €4.

Course overview

What you will learn

Model a choice at the margin, distinguish information problems before and after a contract and explain how a market outcome can differ from a social optimum.

Theory and assumptions

Marginal analysis, expected utility and principal–agent theory. A prediction depends on what each party knows, what it can observe and which alternatives are available.
