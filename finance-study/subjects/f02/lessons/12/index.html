Operate at a loss, suspend, or leave the market?

Use total, variable and fixed costs to choose output and decide on temporary shutdown.

In this lecture

  1. Identify which costs the decision can change
  2. Choose quantity before comparing it with zero
  3. Extend the horizon to entry and exit
  4. Rebuild the calculation
  5. Minimum variable cost and minimum full cost occur at different volumes
  6. Five feasible shifts in a small plant
  7. Independent exam problem
  8. Explain it in an interview
Theory and terminology

The competitive firm's shutdown and exit rules

average variable cost · average total cost · sunk cost

What you should be able to explain

  • Use total, variable and fixed costs to choose output and decide on temporary shutdown.
  • Explain when economic losses imply exit and when entry can erode economic profit.

Identify which costs the decision can change

Short run means at least one input commitment is fixed for the particular decision. Rent already owed even with the doors closed stays in both alternatives. Production can make the loss smaller if sales pay for materials, labor and some rent. A payment called fixed in the accounts can still be avoidable if the company can cancel it.

Economic cost also includes the value of the owner's time and the return available elsewhere on capital. A firm making zero economic profit can pay its bills and deliver that normal return. Accounting earnings can remain positive at the point where economic profit reaches zero.

Choose quantity before comparing it with zero

A price taker accepts the market price on every unit. Compare revenue less total cost at every feasible quantity, including zero. With smooth costs and an interior optimum, produce where rising marginal cost meets price, provided operating beats suspension. With lumpy options, calculate the actual differences.

Average variable cost divides variable cost by units. Price below its minimum cannot cover avoidable production cost at any positive output in the simple model. Average total cost adds fixed cost per unit. Its minimum identifies the price at which the best output covers all economic costs. The two thresholds can be far apart.

Extend the horizon to entry and exit

Suppose identical plants can enter or leave, buyers see the same product, and input prices remain stable. Positive economic profit attracts capacity. An outward supply shift puts pressure on price. Persistent losses lead plants to leave as commitments expire. In the competitive constant-cost model, price tends toward minimum average total cost.

A scarce site, licensing restriction or rising industry input cost changes the conclusion. An analyst should separate the competitive mechanism from the time needed to add a plant or secure a lease. A temporary price spike can support short-run profit without paying back a multiyear expansion.

Method

Build the result

Profit at q = Pq − V(q) − F. AVC = V(q)/q. ATC = [V(q) + F]/q for q > 0.

q is output per week, P is price per unit, V(q) is avoidable operating cost and F is weekly committed cost. AVC and ATC are average variable and total cost per unit. At zero output profit is −F.

  1. Compute total revenue and the cost of each feasible positive output.
  2. Compare the best positive profit with −F to make this week's decision. Equivalently check the largest contribution Pq − V(q).
  3. For a long-run decision, reassess which costs can be avoided and whether prospective price covers the best full-cost alternative.
Worked case

Five feasible shifts in a small plant

The weekly committed charge is €60. At output of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 lots, total variable costs are respectively €44, €70, €102, €148 and €215. At zero lots variable cost is zero. Lots are indivisible.

Working

  1. Minimum AVC is €34 at three lots. The other unit variable costs are €44, €35, €37 and €43 at one, two, four and five lots.
  2. At €38 per lot, weekly profits for zero through five lots are −€60, −€66, −€54, −€48, −€56 and −€85. Three lots minimize the loss.
  3. Three lots earn €114 and incur €102 of variable cost, contributing €12 toward the committed charge. Suspending would forgo this €12.
  4. Minimum ATC is €52 at four lots. Sustained €38 pricing cannot cover the full economic cost, so replacing this unchanged plant at renewal would destroy value.

Decision and interpretation

Keep it open for the week on these facts. A cancellation option on the €60 commitment or an alternative use for the floor changes the decision.

Quick check
At the price that equals minimum AVC, what does the simple model imply this week?

At that output contribution to the fixed commitment is zero. Both choices lose the committed fixed cost.

Written practice

Independent exam problem

A seasonal kiosk can serve either 400 guests or none. At £9 per guest the variable cost for 400 is £3,100. Its £900 licence fee is nonrefundable for this season, but renewal would be voluntary at the same fee. Decide today and for next season, assuming identical demand.

Saved on this device as you type.
Reveal the worked solution
  1. Serving produces £3,600 revenue and £500 contribution. Economic profit after the licence is −£400.
  2. Closing today loses the entire £900. Opening improves the seasonal result by £500.
  3. At renewal all £4,000 of cost is avoidable. At 400 guests, £10 per guest is needed to break even. Decline renewal at £9 if these are the only choices.

Interview practice

Explain it aloud

Why might a plant with a booked loss remain open this week, yet decline to renew its lease?

Saved on this device as you type.
Compare with a spoken answer

This week its output can pay the costs it can still avoid and part of a committed lease. At renewal the lease becomes avoidable too, so expected revenue must cover it as well.

Interviewer’s follow-up

The kiosk can now transfer the licence to another operator for £650 today. Does the original decision survive?

Compare with the follow-up answer

Giving up £650 of resale proceeds becomes a cost of operating. It exceeds the £500 operating contribution, so transferring the licence improves the outcome by £150.
