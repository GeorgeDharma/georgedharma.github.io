Financial Mathematics and the Time Value of Money

Two payments with different dates cannot be added meaningfully until they are translated to a common date. This principle underlies loans, bonds and business valuation.

Subject worked example

A delayed payment

A customer offers €110 one year from now. Equivalent-risk one-year money earns 5%. Compare this with €103 today.

Show the working

Present value is €110/1.05 = €104.76. The later payment is worth about €1.76 more at that rate. Customer default risk could change the comparison.

Course overview

What you will learn

Convert rates consistently, draw a cash-flow timeline, calculate present value and explain when an internal rate of return can mislead.

Theory and assumptions

Discounted cash flow and no-arbitrage. A single discount rate fits a simple comparison when cash flows share the assumed risk and financing conditions.
