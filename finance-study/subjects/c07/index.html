Derivatives and Risk Transfer

A farmer, importer or lender may want to fix a future price. A derivative redistributes uncertainty, and its value depends on exactly what each party must pay in each state.

Subject worked example

A one-step option

A share costs €100 and will end at €120 or €80. A call with strike €100 pays €20 or €0. The one-period financing rate is zero.

Show the working

Hold 0.5 share and borrow €40. Terminal payoffs are €20 and €0. The portfolio costs €50 − €40 = €10, the call’s no-arbitrage price in this model.

Course overview

What you will learn

Derive a forward price, construct a one-step replicating hedge and explain how option and swap payoffs differ from their initial values.

Theory and assumptions

Replication, no-arbitrage, put–call parity and risk-neutral valuation. Simple formulas assume contract conventions, financing and trading opportunities that must be stated.
