Professional workshop · S05

Underwrite the sponsor bid for Larkspur Components

The fund is considering buying 100% of an industrial components company at eight times its €12m normalized entry EBITDA. Build a three-year leveraged buyout model with closing settlement, debt repayments, exit proceeds, downside and a maximum bid consistent with a 20% annual sponsor hurdle. Deliver a complete investment committee recommendation. All cash flows are fictional, denominated in €m and occur at year-end unless described as closing cash.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Private-equity investment associate
What to submit
An LBO investment committee case with a balanced sources-and-uses statement, debt roll-forward, covenant schedule, exit sensitivity and a sponsor-return recommendation.

Illustrative case prepared for this workshop.

From inputs to decision

01

Translate the operating offer into cheque to the seller

The bid multiple prices operations. The selling shareholders and old lender receive different pieces at closing.

  1. Multiply €12m normalized EBITDA by 8 to get €96m enterprise value. Seller share consideration is €96m − €22m existing debt + €6m target cash = €80m.
  2. The old lenders must be repaid €22m. Fees add €3m, so closing uses are €80m + €22m + €3m = €105m. Avoid adding the full €96m operating price to the old debt again.

Seller equity purchase price €80m and total settlement uses €105m.

02

Size sponsor equity and check opening leverage

Only part of the target’s bank balance may fund closing. Relying on the full €6m would leave the factory short of operating cash the next day.

  1. Sweep €6m − €2m = €4m eligible target cash. This and €53m new debt contribute €57m, leaving sponsor equity €105m − €57m = €48m.
  2. Closing net debt equals €53m − €2m retained cash = €51m. Against €12m entry EBITDA, leverage is 4.25 times. At the 4.50 limit net debt headroom is €54m − €51m = €3m.

Sources of €4m target cash, €53m debt and €48m equity reconcile exactly to €105m uses.

03

Build operating cash available for debt service

A debt schedule based on EBITDA can sweep money the factory needs for tax, replacement machinery or stock. The case supplies each operating cash requirement.

  1. Year one CFADS is €13m − €1.4m − €2.6m − €1m = €8m. Year two is €14m − €1.6m − €2.4m − €1m = €9m.
  2. Year three gives €15m − €2m − €2.5m − €0.5m = €10m. Sum €27m over the hold. Interest is paid from this amount in the next schedule and has not already been deducted.

Annual available operating cash before debt service equals €8m, €9m and €10m.

04

Roll debt through mandatory payments and sweeps

The declining loan affects each following year’s interest and determines the sponsor’s exit equity. Calculate on unrounded balances.

  1. Year one interest is 6% × €53m = €3.18m. Pay €3m mandatory principal and sweep €8m − €3.18m − €3m = €1.82m. Closing principal is €48.18m.
  2. Year two interest is €2.8908m and sweep €3.1092m after €3m mandatory repayment. Closing principal is €42.0708m. Year three interest is €2.524248m, sweep €4.475752m and closing principal €34.595048m.

Over the holding period €18.404952m of principal is repaid. Added to interest of €8.595048m, this reconciles to the full €27m CFADS.

05

Test lender protection annually

A sponsor can achieve its expected return and still lose flexibility through a covenant breach. Test the actual period definitions and the retained eligible cash.

  1. Year one net leverage is (€48.18m − €2m)/€13m = 3.55 times. Year two is €40.0708m/€14m = 2.86 times. Year three is €32.595048m/€15m = 2.17 times.
  2. Compare each with the annual 3.75 limit. Year one is the tightest year-end test. At that date allowable net debt is €48.75m, against €46.18m actual, leaving €2.57m headroom.

All base-case leverage tests pass, with the first operating year the priority monitoring period.

06

Calculate exit cash and sponsor return

Headline enterprise sale value is not the sponsor distribution. Net remaining debt and fees must be removed once.

  1. Year-three operating sale price is 8 × €15m = €120m. Exit costs are €2.40m. Equity proceeds are €120m − €2.40m − €34.595048m + €2m = €85.004952m.
  2. Sponsor MOIC is €85.004952m/€48m = 1.77094 times. With one entry outflow and one three-year receipt, annualized return is (1.77094)^(1/3) − 1 = 20.986%.

Base underwriting clears the 20% hurdle by about one percentage point and relies on sustaining an 8.0-times exit multiple.

07

Reverse the bid and break the exit multiple

The fund needs a walk-away amount before competitive tension changes negotiation discipline.

  1. Maximum entry sponsor cheque for a 20% hurdle is €85.004952m/1.20³ = €49.192681m. With debt and other closing balances unchanged, each additional euro of enterprise purchase price adds one euro to entry equity.
  2. The original €96m operating price requires €48m equity, so entry ceiling is €97.192681m. Independently, the €48m cheque needs €82.944m exit equity. Solve 0.98 × multiple × €15m − €32.595048m = €82.944m. The base operations need about 7.860 times at exit.

Set a maximum bid of €97.19m enterprise value on the stated base projections. The operating and exit assumptions warrant retaining the initial €96m offer.

08

Run the downside and deliver the investment view

A lower exit multiple combined with less cash for debt repayment produces a larger equity loss than a single-variable sensitivity.

  1. Year one closing debt remains €48.18m. At €7m second-year CFADS interest is €2.8908m, mandatory principal €3m and sweep €1.1092m, leaving €44.0708m. Year three interest is €2.644248m; €8m cash supports €3m mandatory and €2.355752m sweep, leaving €38.715048m.
  2. At a 7-times exit on €13m EBITDA, enterprise value is €91m and fees €1.82m. Equity proceeds are €91m − €1.82m − €38.715048m + €2m = €52.464952m. MOIC is 1.0930 times and three-year return about 3.01%.

A positive exit cheque is compatible with severe underperformance against the 20% target. Make approval conditional on customer retention, capacity utilization and year-one cash-conversion diligence.

Completed document

Investment committee authorization | Larkspur Components

Decision

Maintain the €96m enterprise bid subject to diligence on forecast demand and year-one cash. At 8.0 times entry the deal requires €48m sponsor equity. Block any offer above €97.19m on current financing and exit assumptions.

Value creation and credit

Base EBITDA grows from €12m to €15m and the €53m term loan amortizes to €34.60m. No multiple expansion is assumed. Year-one leverage headroom is €2.57m after operating cash generation. Daily cash controls should protect the €2m operating floor.

Returns and downside

Base net equity proceeds of €85.00m deliver 1.771 times and 20.99% annually. Slower cash growth and a 7.0-times exit produce only €52.46m and 3.01% annually. The purchase hurdle is sensitive to modest exit rerating; it needs about 7.86 times on base operations.

Conditions and monitoring

Obtain order-book cancellation and pricing evidence, review normalized tax and replacement spending, and confirm that €4m target cash is legally deployable at close. Report cash conversion, headroom and the revised maximum bid whenever operating forecasts change.

Discount the terminal €85.004952m at 22% for three years. Maximum equity is €85.004952m/1.22³, approximately €46.81m. The current €48m cheque exceeds that limit.

The exit bridge already subtracts only €34.60m remaining debt. If the original €53m had stayed outstanding, exit equity would be €18.40m lower. Adding paydown on top of €85.00m would credit the same cash once through lower debt and a second time as an unsupported distribution.
