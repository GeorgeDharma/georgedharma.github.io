Subject worked example

A cash-free buyout

A sponsor acquires a business worth €100m with €60m debt and €40m equity. Five years later enterprise value is €110m and net debt €40m. No interim distributions.

Show the working

Exit equity is €70m. The money multiple is 70/40 = 1.75× and annualized return is 1.75^(1/5) − 1 ≈ 11.84%. Lower debt explains €20m of the €30m equity gain.

Course overview

What you will learn

Build a capitalization table, model a buyout and attribute investor returns to operating changes, debt repayment and the exit price.

Theory and assumptions

Agency theory, staged financing, option-like equity and leveraged buyout returns. Financial leverage changes the distribution of outcomes and can magnify both gains and losses.
