New and expanded lessons

S05.11

Rebuilding the cap table in a down round

Preferred rights and staged-financing lessons cover exit preferences and percentage dilution. The depth notes mention anti-dilution. This chapter reprices the conversion ratio using full-ratchet and weighted-average clauses and reconciles ownership across all shareholders.

  • Compute conversion-price adjustments under two down-round clauses.
  • Reconcile how founder, protected investor and new investor ownership changes.

S05.12

Comparing fund waterfalls and recovering excess carry

The fund economics unit works the preferred-return catch-up and notes that early carry can require repayment. Here two exits are run through two contract structures, followed by a closure reconciliation, escrow release and collection test.

  • Compute interim and final LP and GP distributions for two waterfall designs.
  • Separate the contractual clawback from the cash available to satisfy it.

S05.13

Converting post-money SAFEs into a priced venture round

The existing course prices equity rounds and models staged dilution and liquidation preferences. This chapter reconstructs the capitalization between an early seed instrument and the subsequent priced round, including when the cap shortcut applies.

  • Calculate how much ownership several post-money cap SAFEs imply before new round money.
  • Convert that ownership into actual shares and distinguish a discount-only conversion.

Public benchmarks

MIT Sloan School of Management

15.391 Early Stage Capital, Lecture 2: Term Sheets 101

The lecture develops the investor rights in a term sheet, including preferred conversion, staged financing and how full-ratchet and broad- or narrow-based weighted-average provisions allocate down-round dilution.

Institutional Limited Partners Association

Model Limited Partnership Agreement

ILPA publishes separate whole-of-fund and deal-by-deal model agreements for buyout partnerships, giving LPs and GPs two concrete structures for distributing the same underlying investment proceeds.

Institutional Limited Partners Association

Private Equity Principles, Appendix B: Carry Clawback Best Practice Considerations

The appendix sets out economic and collection issues in returning excess carried interest, including escrow, guarantees, taxes and interim assessments.

Y Combinator

User Guide to the Post-Money Safe

The instrument originator models ownership sold with post-money valuation caps, multiple financings, the later priced investment and the effects of additional dilution.

University of Chicago Polsky Center for Entrepreneurship and Innovation

The University of Chicago SAFE FAQ

The university explains its particular discount-based seed agreement through conversion, equity financing and other triggering events, including how a reduced price determines the number of shares issued.

Singapore Academy of Law and Singapore Venture & Private Capital Association

Venture Capital Investment Model Agreements 2.0

The Singapore-law suite separates term sheets and convertible notes used at Series A from the pre-Series A Convertible Agreement Regarding Equity. It shows why conversion mechanics must be read from the local instrument.
