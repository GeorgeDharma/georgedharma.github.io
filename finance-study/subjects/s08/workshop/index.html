Professional workshop · S08

A market-risk limit breach on Friday's funding calendar

At Thursday's close, a fictional euro bank must decide whether to keep its present trading book, buy one-day protection or cut positions before Friday settlement. The risk committee expects a signed limit exception report, a tail-loss attribution, a dated funding check and an independent challenge to the desk's VaR model. Five teaching states jointly price the entire book. Losses below are positive and all money is in EUR millions. Treat probabilities as stipulated model inputs.

InputsWalkthroughReconciled exhibitCompleted documentYour turn
Your role
Independent bank risk analyst presenting to the asset and liability committee
What to submit
Prepare the one-day market-risk exception and liquidity escalation note. The trading head needs a hedge authorization, treasury must meet tomorrow's payments, and the committee must know precisely which limit or stress shortfall remains after the hedge.

From inputs to decision

01

Build the distribution from joint desk losses

Whole-book probabilities must match across desks. Adding independently selected worst losses would destroy the cross-desk dependence supplied in the scenario table.

  1. Sum the three desk amounts in each row. Portfolio losses are €0m, €3m, €5m, €9m and €20m. The five probabilities sum to 100%. The cumulative distribution is 90%, 94%, 97%, 99% and 100%.
  2. As a control calculate expected one-day loss of 0.04 × 3 + 0.03 × 5 + 0.02 × 9 + 0.01 × 20 = €0.65m. Keep this average visibly distinct from the aggregated tail.

Save the scenario series and the table before applying the hedge. Independent risk can then reproduce the pretrade numbers from five rows and compare like-for-like positions.

02

Find the 95% boundary and exact tail mean

The 3% rates state crosses the percentile boundary, so using its entire mass in the tail would average six percent of scenarios.

  1. Cumulative probability is only 94% at €3m and reaches 97% at €5m. VaR therefore equals €5m under the specified convention.
  2. For expected shortfall use 1% at €20m, 2% at €9m and exactly 2% at €5m. Normalize (0.01 × 20 + 0.02 × 9 + 0.02 × 5)/0.05 to obtain €9.6m. The current book exceeds the €9m internal limit by €0.6m.

An exception should open for €0.6m. Using VaR instead would conceal the supplied breach and fail to quantify how severe the outcomes beyond the boundary are.

03

Attribute risk using the portfolio's tail states

Committee members need to see which desks contribute when the whole bank is in trouble. Apply the same tail masses to every desk.

  1. The €3.4m rates contribution is (0.01 × 8 + 0.02 × 0.5 + 0.02 × 4)/0.05. Currency contributes (0.01 × 7 + 0.02 × 5 + 0.02 × 0.5)/0.05 = €3.6m.
  2. Credit contributes (0.01 × 5 + 0.02 × 3.5 + 0.02 × 0.5)/0.05 = €2.6m. The components reconcile exactly to €9.6m. Ranking each desk's own worst rows would answer another management question.

Currency is the largest contribution in the given common tail even though the rates desk records the largest single-desk loss in the combined state.

04

Price the protection and repeat the test

The proposed hedge is a contingent receipt and a certain premium. Adding the premium in only the payout scenario would understate cost.

  1. Gross of premium, protection changes only the €20m combined loss to €16m, with row ranking unchanged. The probability-normalized tail benefit is 0.01 × €4m / 0.05 = €0.8m.
  2. The €0.2m premium increases losses in all states. Revised losses are €0.2m, €3.2m, €5.2m, €9.2m and €16.2m. Expected shortfall is €9.6m − €0.8m + €0.2m = €9.0m and VaR is €5.2m.

The hedge reaches the limit exactly and leaves no modelling cushion. The premium can raise the threshold even as protection reduces severity farther into the tail.

05

Check timing before signing the hedge

The valuation scenario can recognize a protection receivable before the bank can use it as settlement cash.

  1. The thirty-day liquidity calculation recognizes only €75m of the €80m inflows. Net stressed outflows are €25m, and the base published illustration is €30m / €25m = 120% before the proposed premium. This aggregate view does not schedule Friday's payment.
  2. Friday resources are €9m cash plus a separate €4m line, or €13m against €14m obligations. Paying the premium requires another €0.2m. The protected book therefore needs at least €1.2m additional cash before cutoff, even in the state in which it will receive €4m on day two.

Treasury must pre-position freely available same-day cash or agree a payment deferral of at least €1.2m. Document the settlement confirmation and avoid counting already pledged resources twice.

06

Test whether the worst observed state remains financeable

A one-day ES limit and a capital stress floor are distinct controls. An authorization addressing one can still leave an unresolved capital requirement.

  1. Required capital is the greater of 10% × €600m and 4% × €1,500m, both €60m. Starting capital of €75m gives €15m headroom. Under the unhedged combined loss, capital falls to €55m, €5m below the internal floors.
  2. With the hedge and its premium the combined loss is €16.2m, leaving €58.8m capital, still €1.2m below the required €60m at fixed denominators. Market valuation of the receivable and the timing of its eventual cash are deliberately evaluated separately.

The transaction reduces the model-tail exception and much of the capital stress gap. It cannot justify claiming the whole book passes the capital scenario.

07

Challenge the historical model before closing the issue

The scenario exercise is a prospective measure. Previous 99% VaR forecasts are a separate source of evidence about the desk's risk process.

  1. For 250 days at a 1% exception rate the expected count is 2.5. Eight observed gives p-hat = 0.032 and Kupiec unconditional-coverage LR approximately 7.73, above the usual asymptotic 3.84 reference at 5%. Rare-event samples warrant an exact-binomial check alongside the approximation.
  2. Four exceptions in one five-day window also challenge the model's speed of adjustment. Request the frozen opening-book P&L, timestamped marks, changing positions and factor volatility during that window. A count test alone does not use exception order.

Keep model remediation open. Approve the hedge only alongside dated funding and a separate capital action or formally escalated stress exception.

Completed document

ALCO market-risk limit exception and funding direction

Exposure

One-day portfolio ES exceeds the €9m internal threshold by €0.6m. Portfolio-tail contributions are rates €3.4m, FX €3.6m and credit €2.6m. The combined state costs €20m unprotected.

Trading authorization

Purchase the €4m combined-state payoff for no more than €0.2m only after settlement terms and counterparty capacity are confirmed. At that premium modeled ES is exactly €9m. Re-run the joint states after any quote or position change.

Liquidity action

Pre-position at least €1.2m before Friday cutoff, beyond the documented €9m cash and €4m line. No day-two receipt may be counted toward that deadline. Treasury will return a time-stamped funding confirmation.

Capital and governance

Protected stressed capital remains €1.2m below the internal floor. Escalate the stress gap for a genuine risk reduction, eligible capital addition or formally approved exception. Retain the VaR backtest review until the clustered exceptions are explained on frozen positions.

If the same hedge pays €5m in the combined state and still costs €0.2m, what is revised 95% ES?

The additional €1m payoff saves 0.01 × €1m/0.05 = €0.2m in expected shortfall because combined remains in the tail. New ES is €8.8m.

Task 2

What is the largest certain premium for the stated €4m protection that still meets the €9m ES ceiling?

See the full solution

Before premium the protected ES is €9.6m − €0.8m = €8.8m. Every state absorbs the certain premium, so it adds one-for-one to ES. The ceiling leaves €0.2m.

Task 3

The credit desk cuts the combined-state credit loss by €2m without changing any other state, premium or denominator. What capital remains in the protected combined state?

See the full solution

Loss falls from €16.2m to €14.2m. Deducting from €75m leaves €60.8m, now €0.8m above the €60m minimum.

Task 4

Why must treasury and independent model validation stay involved even if the tail measure passes?

Saved on this device as you type.
See the full solution

The day-two payoff cannot fund Friday. The defined 99% backtest also has too many, clustered exceptions, and compliance at exactly €9m offers no buffer for estimation error. Require dated cash evidence and independent frozen-book analysis rather than treating one scenario hedge as a cure for both.
