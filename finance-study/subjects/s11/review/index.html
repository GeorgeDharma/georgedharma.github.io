New and expanded lessons

S11.11

Sovereign exchange voting and collective action clauses

S11.06 mentions collective action clauses and values a restructuring. There is a further execution question about eligible votes, aggregation across bond series, and when identical financial terms can or cannot bind dissenting holders.

  • Reconcile eligible principal and consent across series under different voting structures.
  • Explain why the voting threshold and the economic haircut require different evidence.

S11.12

Debt-office strategy, maturity and time to refixing

S11.04 analyzes a rollover gap and S11.09 explains when a rate shock reaches the budget. This chapter builds medium-term debt-office indicators from instrument-level terms, then costs a decision to exchange short exposure for fixed-rate maturity.

  • Calculate average time to maturity, average time to refixing and one-year exposure shares.
  • Translate a rate shock into both first-year interest cash and a later annualized run rate.
