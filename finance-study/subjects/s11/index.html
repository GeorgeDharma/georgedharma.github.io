Sovereign Debt and Emerging-Market Finance

A government can refinance in its own currency or owe foreign currency it cannot issue. Sovereign finance examines the interaction among debt arithmetic, policy credibility and market access.

Debt arithmetic

Debt starts at 60% of output. Nominal interest is 5%, nominal output growth 2% and the primary surplus 1% of current output. Assume no other adjustments.

Show the working

Next debt ratio is (1.05/1.02) × 60% − 1% = 60.76%. A surplus can coexist with a rising ratio when interest outpaces nominal growth.

Course overview

What you will learn

Reconcile a debt path, quantify refinancing and currency risk, and defend a country comparison using both accounting and institutional evidence.

Theory and assumptions

Intertemporal government budget constraint, debt dynamics, rollover crises and sovereign default incentives. Debt sustainability depends on growth, rates, fiscal balances and the structure of obligations.
