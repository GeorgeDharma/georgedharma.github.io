Harbour Instruments’ first-quarter close

A ferry operator’s reserved-seat contract

A contract-pricing sheet with a reservation-price floor and cancellation limits, accompanied by a commercial decision memorandum to the director and a separate quantified community impact paragraph.

Purchasing an industrial heat pump without breaching treasury’s reserve

A capital expenditure approval sheet with a dated net present value calculation, a liability-matching funding schedule, a liquidity test and a recommendation for the capital committee.

Financial Mathematics and the Time Value of Money
F04 · Credit analytics economist

Does a collections reminder pay for itself?

A committee evidence note with a paired-effects workpaper, a confidence interval, an economic break-even rate, a rollout decision and sensitivity to a possible data contamination.

Probability, Statistics and Inference

Signing off a dual-currency portfolio after a split and a replayed dividend

A portfolio NAV sign-off, a working Python implementation, return attribution and a control memorandum explaining the treatment of corrected quotes, duplicate transaction IDs and end-of-day contributions. NAV means net asset value, the euro value of holdings plus cash here.

Core finance

C01 · Country economist on a central-bank financial stability team

Cordela's reserve floor before the bond maturity

A reserve-adequacy briefing for the monetary policy and financial stability committee, including a balance-of-payments reconciliation, quarterly foreign-currency cash ladder, intervention trigger and a distinct corporate currency-mismatch observation.

Open-Economy Macroeconomics and Monetary Systems
C02 · Group treasury analyst on a corporate FX dealing desk

Hedge a French export contract without committing its unsigned extension

A treasury hedge ticket and a signed-style recommendation to the finance director, with a maturity-equivalent net-euro scenario grid, funding comparison, instrument quantities, premium budget and monitoring instructions.

Foreign Exchange and Multinational Financial Management
C03 · Corporate development associate preparing a capital-expenditure and funding submission

Approve a sorting line and size its four-year loan

A signed-style capital allocation memorandum and a four-year operating, financing and lender-coverage exhibit for the investment committee and treasurer. All case terms are fictional.

Corporate Finance and Capital Structure
C04 · Financial institutions group valuation analyst supporting an investment director

Set a disciplined bid for a minority stake in a regional bank

A financial institutions valuation committee paper giving a price ceiling payable to the seller, a bank equity roll-forward, independent dividend and residual-income cross-checks, a capital floor test, and an exit position if negotiations fail.

Company and Equity Valuation
C05 · Institutional investment analyst

A liquidity reserve and a global equity allocation for a university endowment

Submit an investment policy implementation memorandum to trustees, with investable euro notionals, an explicit currency trade, a risk budget and an executable review dashboard. The custodian needs clear distinction between cash held for grants and resources available for collateral.

Investments, Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing
C06 · Fixed-income asset–liability portfolio manager

A two-date euro annuity book and an executable cash-flow match

Deliver an asset–liability management trade ticket to dealing and a signed-off surplus-risk reconciliation to the asset–liability committee. Use the liability dates, legal currency and settlement convention consistently so the insurer can actually make the two promised payments.

Fixed Income and the Term Structure of Interest Rates
C07 · Corporate treasury derivatives analyst

A six-month dollar hedge with uncertain export volume and limited euro collateral

Submit a hedge recommendation, dealing ticket and treasury liquidity escalation note to the CFO. Distinguish contracted cash from forecast sales, gross derivative payoffs from net proceeds, and terminal protection from the cash needed to keep the hedge open.

Derivatives and Risk Transfer
C08 · Bank asset–liability management analyst

Approve a rate hedge and a thirty-day funding plan for a commercial bank

Prepare an ALCO decision paper for the treasurer, chief risk officer and board risk committee. The dealing desk needs the hedge direction and size. Treasury needs the pledged and sold securities reconciled without double counting. Capital management needs the minimum remediation and a proposed operating buffer.

Banking and Financial Intermediation
C09 · Equity capital markets associate advising the issuer and the board’s pricing committee

Price the flotation of Marais Instruments

A pricing-committee memorandum, a share-allocation schedule and a closing funds flow, including a decision for the issuer and the selling shareholder.

Acquire the depot network or license its routing system?

A buyer-side investment committee recommendation with a standalone valuation, incremental synergy schedule, sources and uses, debt covenant test, EPS bridge, threshold price and downside decision.

Mergers and Acquisitions
S02 · Corporate credit analyst

Credit committee submission for an exporter’s equipment purchase and seasonal line

Submit a completed sanction memorandum and a reconciled capacity and collateral exhibit to the delegated credit committee. The relationship manager will use its conditions in the offer, and loan operations will use the same figures to determine funds available.

Lending and Credit Analysis
S03 · Transaction services senior analyst

Buy-side quality of earnings and completion accounts for Bracken Service

A quality-of-earnings schedule, a cash-conversion check, a net-debt and working-capital bridge and an exceptions memorandum to the deal partner.

Financial Due Diligence and Transaction Accounting
S04 · Restructuring adviser to an ad hoc creditor committee

Restructure a cold-storage operator before the second Friday

A 13-week liquidity schedule, entity-by-entity recovery waterfalls and a term-sheet recommendation the creditor committee can put to a vote.

Corporate Distress and Restructuring
S05 · Private-equity investment associate

Underwrite the sponsor bid for Larkspur Components

An LBO investment committee case with a balanced sources-and-uses statement, debt roll-forward, covenant schedule, exit sensitivity and a sponsor-return recommendation.

Private Equity, Venture Capital and Leveraged Finance
S06 · Group treasury analyst preparing the dealing and liquidity instructions

Fund payroll and hedge an exporter’s documentary-credit receipt

A treasury cash-position report, approved-deal ticket and independent settlement checklist, followed by a liquidity escalation note to the group treasurer.

International Treasury and Trade Finance
S07 · Regional FP&A analyst supporting the operating director and group controller

The quarter that grew revenue and missed profit

Submit the management-accounting close bridge and a priced special-order recommendation. The group controller uses the reconciliation to release the pack. The sales director uses the quote floor and treasury conditions when negotiating. All euro amounts exclude taxes and financing charges unless stated.

Management Accounting, Planning and Control
S08 · Independent bank risk analyst presenting to the asset and liability committee

A market-risk limit breach on Friday's funding calendar

Prepare the one-day market-risk exception and liquidity escalation note. The trading head needs a hedge authorization, treasury must meet tomorrow's payments, and the committee must know precisely which limit or stress shortfall remains after the hedge.

Financial Risk Management and Regulation
S09 · Buy-side execution analyst reviewing a portfolio manager's equity order

The 500 shares missing from the broker's execution report

Submit an execution-quality report to the head trader, portfolio manager and best-execution committee. The completed record must distinguish the manager's decision delay, desk implementation, spread and market movement, and the evidence needed to challenge one missed fill. No external market data are needed.

Market Microstructure, Trading and Settlement
S10 · Project-finance credit analyst on an infrastructure lender's underwriting team

Credit approval for a three-year district solar loan

Deliver a project credit application and indicative facility terms for the infrastructure credit committee. The cash-flow exhibit must reconcile opening debt, annual interest and principal, debt-service coverage, reserve top-ups and distributable cash, including final reserve release.

Project, Development and Sustainable Finance
S11 · Sovereign debt-management office analyst briefing the finance minister

A treasury financing plan with a dollar maturity wall

Submit the annual borrowing requirement, cash and currency financing matrix, debt-stock roll-forward and contingency decision to the debt-management director. Identify confirmed resources separately from potential financing and central-bank reserves, and record the bank–sovereign exposure that should be monitored before increasing domestic issuance.

Sovereign Debt and Emerging-Market Finance
S12 · Portfolio performance and investment governance officer for a defined-benefit pension trustee

Harbour Engineers Pension Plan annual investment committee paper

Submit a signed committee paper with a reconciled custody and actuarial exhibit, return attribution, a trade instruction with a cash reserve, and an independently reviewed proxy instruction. The trustees can adopt or amend each decision at the meeting.

Investment Management and Performance Evaluation
