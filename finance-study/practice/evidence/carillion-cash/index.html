United Kingdom · Parliamentary inquiry, 2018

Carillion and the source of operating cash

Reconstruct how cash conversion changes when a supplier-finance facility is treated as borrowing.

What the record shows

The parliamentary inquiry examined an arrangement under which a bank paid suppliers early and Carillion settled later. For 2013–2016 the report cites £509m of operating cash inflow and £501m of group operating profit. It discusses an alternative analysis that reclassified £472m of inflows associated with the facility as financing. The Financial Reporting Council told the inquiry that the contractual terms matter to classification.

  • House of Commons joint inquiry into Carillion, paragraphs 91–94

Work through the figures

Figures from the inquiry. Both percentages use the same denominator. The alternative is an analytical reclassification.

Cash conversion asks how much operating profit appeared as cash from operations over the same period. Moving a flow from financing to operating makes operating cash look stronger while leaving the physical change in the company’s bank balance exactly the same. For a lender, the important question is whether customers generated the cash or a financing provider advanced it and can later demand repayment.

Test your reasoning

A separate teaching borrower reports operating cash of £18m, including a £6m rise attributable to a similar facility. It pays £4m in maintenance investment. Annual principal and interest due are £10m. Calculate coverage under both cash classifications. What evidence could validate the adjustment?

Compare your answer

With reported operating cash, available cash is 18 − 4 = £14m and coverage is 14 / 10 = 1.40×. Reclassifying the £6m leaves 18 − 6 − 4 = £8m available and coverage of 0.80×. This exercise assumes taxes are already reflected and the £10m debt service is the same in both views. Inspect the facility contract and bank confirmations, reconcile financed invoices and payment dates, then verify the cash-flow bridge. A ratio calculated under a loan covenant also needs the covenant’s own definition.
