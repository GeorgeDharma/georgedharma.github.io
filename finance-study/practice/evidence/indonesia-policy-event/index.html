Indonesia · October 2023

Bank Indonesia and the event-study benchmark

Move from a dated rate announcement to a testable question about listed bank shares.

What the record shows

At its October 18–19, 2023 meeting Bank Indonesia raised the seven-day reverse repo rate by 25 basis points to 6.00%. The bank connected the decision to rupiah stability and imported inflation. That statement establishes the decision and its stated rationale. Investors may already have anticipated all or part of it.

  • Bank Indonesia, October 2023 Board of Governors decision

All returns in this table are invented for the exercise, expressed in a common currency. Assume synchronized closes, alpha = 0 and market beta = 1 for both benchmarks.

An abnormal return is observed return less the return expected under the chosen model. Under the table’s assumptions that expected return equals the benchmark return. The local benchmark removes common local market movement, including part of any common policy response. The regional benchmark asks a different question about performance relative to another market. Neither choice, by itself, establishes a causal effect.

Test your reasoning

Calculate the three-day cumulative abnormal return against both benchmarks. Explain why the positive result disappears. Name two checks you would make before using actual Indonesian bank data for this design.

Compare your answer

The local daily gaps are +0.2, +0.6 and +0.2 percentage points. They sum to +1.0 point. The regional gaps are +0.3, −0.7 and +0.4 points, summing to 0.0. The apparent positive performance disappears with the regional benchmark; the calculation has not changed the bank’s observed returns. Verify the actual release time relative to the trading session, check overlapping earnings or regulatory news, and estimate each market sensitivity before the event. Define currency treatment, holidays and ownership or size controls before examining group differences.
