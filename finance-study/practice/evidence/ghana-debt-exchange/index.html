Ghana · February 2023

Ghana’s debt exchange and the value of a promise

Separate the amount printed on a new bond from the economic value of waiting for its cash.

What the record shows

Ghana settled its domestic exchange on February 21, 2023. The Ministry of Finance reported 16 series of new bonds. For an accepted holder, the principal of the new bonds included eligible principal tendered plus any accrued interest payable. Allocation depended on the holder’s category. This gives an analyst a starting amount to reconcile before comparing payment dates and coupons.

  • Ghana Ministry of Finance, settlement of the domestic debt exchange

Work through the figures

Illustrative creditor, amounts in Ghana cedisCash dueValue today at 10%
Old claim assumed collectible in full today100 principal + 5 accrued interest105.00
New claim, end of year 14.20 coupon3.82
New claim, end of year 24.20 coupon3.47
New claim, end of year 34.20 coupon + 105 principal82.04

The two claims and the assumed discount rate are classroom examples. They do not reproduce the terms of any of Ghana’s 16 series.

A promise to pay 105 several years from now has a different value from receiving 105 today. Discount the payment in each year by (1 + required return) raised to the number of years you must wait. At a 10% return, the new payments are worth about 89.33 today. Dividing that by the old claim’s assumed value of 105 gives a recovery of 85.08%, equivalent to a 14.92% loss in value. There is no reduction in the stated 105 principal in this example.

Test your reasoning

If the required return falls to 4%, what is the value of the new 4% coupon bond? The old claim was assumed to be payable immediately in full. How would the comparison change if the likely alternative payment, valued today, were just 80?

Compare your answer

At a 4% required return, annual coupons of 4.20 exactly compensate for waiting on 105 principal. Discounting 4.20, 4.20 and 109.20 gives 105. In the original 10% scenario, the new package is worth 89.33. Against an alternative worth 80, exchanging improves the creditor’s estimated value by 9.33 cedis. An analyst has to document the relevant alternative and discount rate to make an economic haircut interpretable.
