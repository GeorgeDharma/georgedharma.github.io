Career applications

See how finance professionals bring several subjects together to answer one question. Each example links to the lessons behind the decision.

Professional application

Investment banking and M&A

A buyer is considering a smaller competitor.

Build both businesses as independent firms, estimate achievable savings, and show the highest offer the buyer can justify. The deal team needs to know who keeps the gains under cash and share offers.

What the team needs

A defensible price and deal structure.

Professional application

Commercial and corporate lending

An exporter requests a higher seasonal credit line.

Trace when it pays suppliers and receives customers’ money. Recalculate coverage if collections slow, and identify collateral whose proceeds the bank could actually recover.

What the team needs

A credit decision with a priced facility and monitored covenants.

Professional application

Transaction services and restructuring

A target reports rising profit as available cash falls.

Reconcile the proposed earnings adjustments to cash, make a short-term payment forecast, and compare recoveries if creditors give the business more time.

What the team needs

Normalized earnings, liquidity needs and creditor recoveries.

Professional application

Private equity and venture capital

An investment fund must decide how much ownership to buy.

Translate operating forecasts into exit proceeds. Stress a lower exit price and trace who receives cash under the financing contract before comparing the investment with alternatives.

What the team needs

A buyout or venture investment with investor payoff schedules.

Professional application

Corporate finance and FP&A

A finance director has to choose between opening a plant and expanding a distributor network.

Build forecasts from sales volumes and unit costs. Compare incremental cash, investment timing and the cost of capital, then explain the assumptions most likely to overturn the decision.

What the team needs

An operating forecast and capital allocation decision.

Professional application

Treasury and trade finance

A group sells in rupiah, pays suppliers in dollars and reports in euros.

Map cash in each currency by date. Match receipts to payments, price the remaining hedge, and test what happens to liquidity when a shipment is delayed.

What the team needs

A multicurrency funding, payment and hedging plan.

Professional application

Investment and wealth management

A client needs a stable income stream and can tolerate only limited losses.

Translate the request into a spending horizon and a risk budget. Compare candidate portfolios against the actual payments and explain where performance came from.

What the team needs

A mandate, allocation and performance attribution.

Professional application

Global markets, execution and risk

A trading desk needs to reduce risk before a bond issue settles.

Identify the rate, spread and execution exposures separately. Compare hedges under a stressed price move, account for market depth and reconcile the resulting trades.

What the team needs

An execution study and a risk-limit decision.

Professional application

Development and infrastructure finance

A lender is reviewing a solar plant funded in two currencies.

Size repayments against stressed project cash flow. Identify which contractual party absorbs delays or currency movements and how the claimed public benefit will be measured.

What the team needs

Project sizing, risk allocation and development impact.

Professional application

Country research and sovereign risk

A sovereign analyst is asked whether a government can refinance next year.

Map the maturity and currency of debt before forecasting the long-run debt ratio. Quantify the largest near-term funding gap under a weaker currency or higher interest rate.

What the team needs

A country comparison and reproducible debt stress test.

Professional application

Quantitative investment research

An asset manager wants to know whether a published signal survives trading costs.

Write down the trading rule before testing it. Enforce data availability by date, compare with a simple benchmark and measure performance in a genuinely untouched test period.

What the team needs

A reproducible research paper and cost-aware backtest.

Professional application

Pricing and model validation

A bank introduces a new option-pricing model.

Build an independent simple price, check convergence and boundary cases, then explain the hedging losses that remain when markets violate the model assumptions.

What the team needs

Independent pricing, convergence tests and hedge validation.

Professional application

Equity and debt capital markets

What price funds the company and leaves an executable order book?

An origination analyst reconciles share ownership, stress-tests investor demand and writes the committee recommendation. For a bond issue the analyst also builds the spread bridge from outstanding comparables.

What the team needs

A pricing recommendation, allocation rationale and reconciliation of issuer proceeds.
