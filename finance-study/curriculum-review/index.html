How to read the review

For each of the original subjects, we compared published teaching outcomes, course schedules and technical examples with the lecture text, methods, workshops and assessments. A passing mention did not count as teaching a method. A gap earned a full lecture when learners needed to derive a result, make a professional decision or answer a new exam question.

The comparisons below document selected benchmark topics. A university syllabus establishes course scope. An exchange, standard setter, researcher or professional manual supplies the detail for a particular method or market.
